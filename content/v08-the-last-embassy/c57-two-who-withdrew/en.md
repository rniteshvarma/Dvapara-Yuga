::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,dhrishtadyumna,arjuna,krishna light=dhrishtadyumna}

When [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) heard that Bhishma led the Kurus, he called his brothers and Krishna. *Our first encounter will be with our grandsire.* He set his seven leaders over the seven armies: [Drupada](drupada), [Virata](virata), Satyaki, Dhrishtadyumna, [Dhrishtaketu](dhrishtaketu), Shikhandi, and Sahadeva of Magadha. Over all of them he set [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna); over Dhrishtadyumna, [Arjuna](arjuna); and Krishna would be Arjuna's guide and drive his horses. {#p1 5.158}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=balarama,yudhishthira,krishna light=balarama}

Then [Balarama](balarama) came into the camp in blue silk, with Akrura, Samba, Uddhava, [Pradyumna](pradyumna) and other Vrishnis around him, his eyes red at the corners from drink. Everyone rose; he sat on Yudhishthira's seat beside him and looked at Krishna. {#p2 5.158}

The slaughter was fate and could not be stopped, he said. He had told Krishna again and again, in private, to treat both sides alike, for Duryodhana was as much their kin as the Pandavas; but Krishna, for Arjuna's sake, had not listened. {#p3 5.158}

> As regards myself, I dare not cast my eyes on the world without Krishna (on my side). {#q1 5.158}
> — Balarama

Bhima and Duryodhana were both his pupils with the mace, and he loved them equally. He could not watch the Kurus destroyed. He would go and bathe at the holy places on the Sarasvati. He took his leave, stopped Krishna from following, and set out. {#p4 5.158}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=rukmi,rukmini,krishna light=rukmi}

About then [Rukmi](rukmi) arrived with an army, son of Bhishmaka, king of the south. He held the bow Vijaya, once Indra's, equal to Gandiva and to Krishna's Sharnga, which he had learned to use from a Kimpurusha named Drona on Gandhamadana, not the Kuru teacher. Long ago, when Krishna carried off his sister [Rukmini](rukmini), Rukmi had sworn not to come home without killing him, had been beaten, and out of shame had built a new city, Bhojakata, where he fell, rather than return to Kundina. {#p5 5.159}

He came now, he said, to please Vasudeva, and spoke to Arjuna before them all. If Arjuna was afraid, Rukmi would help; there was no one his equal; let Arjuna name the enemy, Drona, Kripa, Bhishma or Karna, and he would kill him. {#p6 5.159}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=arjuna,rukmi,duryodhana light=arjuna}

Arjuna smiled. Who had helped him against the Gandharvas, or the gods at Khandava, or the Nivatakavachas, or the Kurus at Virata's city? {#p7 5.159}

> O thou of mighty arms, I am not afraid, nor have I any need of thy assistance. Go therefore, or stay, as it pleaseth or suiteth thee. {#q2 5.159}
> — Arjuna

Rukmi took his army, vast as the sea, to Duryodhana and said the same thing; and Duryodhana, proud of his own strength, turned him away too. {#p8 5.159}

> Thus, O king, two persons withdrew from the battle, viz., Rohini's son (Rama) of Vrishni's race and king Rukmi. {#q3 5.159}
