::scene{#s1 frame=krishna,i239_agni light=krishna}

The falling mountain drove the last creatures of Khandava out into the open, Danavas, Rakshasas, Nagas, wolves, bears, elephants, tigers, lions, deer, buffalo and birds, and there they found the forest burning on every side and Krishna and Arjuna waiting. {#p1 1.230}

[Krishna](krishna) hurled his discus, and it came back to his hand each time. No one among the gods could defeat the two of them, and when the gods saw that the forest could not be saved, they withdrew. {#p2 1.230}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=indra,takshaka,krishna,arjuna light=indra}

Then a voice from the sky spoke to [Indra](indra). {#p3 1.230}

> Thy friend Takshaka, that chief of snakes, hath not been slain! Before the conflagration commenced in Khandava he had journeyed to Kurukshetra. {#q1 1.230}
> — a voice from the sky

Krishna and Arjuna could not be beaten by anyone, the voice said; they were Nara and Narayana, the ancient gods. The burning of Khandava had been ordained by fate. {#p4 1.230}

Indra knew it was true. He let go of his anger and went back to heaven, and the gods followed him. Krishna and Arjuna roared like lions, and went on with the burning, and no creature escaped. {#p5 1.230}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=i239_agni light=i239_agni}

Agni drank rivers of fat, and his flames rose high without a wisp of smoke. His eyes were copper, his tongue blazed, the hair of his head was fire, and he was happy. {#p6 1.230}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=i7257_maya,krishna,arjuna,i239_agni light=i7257_maya}

Then Krishna saw an Asura running from Takshaka's house. It was [Maya](i7257_maya), the brother of Namuchi. Agni, with the wind for his charioteer, came after him with matted hair of flame, roaring; Krishna raised his discus. {#p7 1.230}

> Run to me, O Arjuna, and protect me! {#q2 1.230}
> — Maya

*Fear not!* said [Arjuna](arjuna), and those two words, the epic says, gave Maya back his life. Because Arjuna had promised him safety, Krishna lowered his discus and Agni did not burn him. {#p8 1.230}

***

::scene{#s5 frame=i239_agni,i7257_maya light=i239_agni}

Agni burned the forest of Khandava for fifteen days. And of all its creatures he spared six: Ashvasena the snake, Maya the Asura, and four birds called Sarngakas. {#p9 1.230}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=1.230}
**Maya's debt.** Maya owes Arjuna his life, and he will want to repay it. He is a great architect of the Asuras, and the repayment will be a building: the assembly hall of Indraprastha, where the next part of the story begins and where, in time, everything will go wrong.
::
