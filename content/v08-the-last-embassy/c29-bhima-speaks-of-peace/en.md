::scene{#s1 frame=krishna,yudhishthira light=krishna}

[Krishna](krishna) gave Yudhishthira his own view. A life of renunciation is not a Kshatriya's duty; victory or death in battle is. As long as Yudhishthira was mild, the Kauravas would keep his kingdom. They had made him wear a loincloth, and dragged Draupadi in by the hair calling her *cow, cow!*, and felt no remorse. Krishna would go, and before all the kings he would show Yudhishthira's virtues and Duryodhana's vices. But he expected war. Birds and beasts screamed at dusk; fire showed strange colours. Let the army make ready. {#p1 5.73}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhima,krishna light=bhima}

Then, to everyone's surprise, [Bhima](bhima) spoke for peace. Speak gently to Duryodhana, he said; do not threaten him. He named the kings in every age who had destroyed their own kin, and said Duryodhana was one of them. {#p2 5.74}

> All of us, O Krishna, would rather in humiliation follow Duryodhana submissively, but, oh, let not the Bharatas be annihilated. {#q1 5.74}
> — Bhima

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,bhima light=krishna}

It was *as unexpected as if the hills had lost their weight and fire had become cold*. Krishna laughed, and fanned him like a breeze on a fire. Bhima, who lay awake all night face down, who sighed like smoking fire, who had sworn by the rising and setting sun to kill Duryodhana with his mace: was he afraid now? {#p3 5.75}

> Alas, like a eunuch, thou dost not display any sign indicative of manliness in thee. {#q2 5.75}
> — Krishna

Bhima rose *like a steed of high metal*. Did Krishna not know him? If heaven and earth crashed together, he could hold them apart with his arms. No one he grasped could get free, not the Himalaya, nor the ocean, nor Indra. {#p4 5.76}

> It is only for the sake of compassion, O slayer of Madhu, that I am for displaying goodwill to the foe. {#q3 5.76}
> — Bhima

***

::scene{#s4 frame=krishna,bhima,arjuna light=krishna}

Krishna said he had spoken only to learn Bhima's mind, out of affection. The results of human acts are uncertain; one should act knowing that success comes from destiny and effort together, and be neither crushed by failure nor elated by success. He would go to Dhritarashtra in the morning. If there was war, the burden would rest on Bhima, and on Arjuna. {#p5 5.77}

> In case of war happening, I will certainly be the driver of Vibhatsu's car, for that, indeed, is Dhananjaya's wish and not that I myself am not desirous of fighting. {#q4 5.77}
> — Krishna
