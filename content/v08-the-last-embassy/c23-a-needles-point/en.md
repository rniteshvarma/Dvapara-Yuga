::scene{#s1 frame=dhrishtadyumna,yudhishthira,sanjaya light=dhrishtadyumna}

Who was urging the Pandavas on? asked the king. [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna), said [Sanjaya](sanjaya). He would take on all the angry kings himself, *like a whale seizing little fishes from the water*, and hold off Bhishma, Drona, Karna and the rest *like the bank resisting the swelling sea*. [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) told him the Pandavas and Panchalas depended on him. And Dhrishtadyumna sent the Kurus a message: before Arjuna kills you, let some good man go and beg Yudhishthira to take back his kingdom. {#p1 5.57}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=dhritarashtra,duryodhana light=dhritarashtra}

[Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra) begged his son. War is never praised. Half the earth was enough for him and all his followers; let him give the Pandavas their share. No one wanted this war, not Bahlika, not Bhishma, not Drona, not Ashwatthama, not Kripa, not Shalya. It was Karna, Duhshasana and Shakuni leading him into it. {#p2 5.58}

[Duryodhana](duryodhana) said he did not need any of them. He and Karna would perform the sacrifice of battle, with Yudhishthira as the victim: his chariot the altar, his sword and mace the ladles, his arrows the sacred grass, fame the clarified butter. {#p3 5.58}

> O venerable one, I will not surrender to the Pandavas even that much of land which may be covered by the sharp point of a needle. {#q1 5.58}
> — Duryodhana

***

::scene{#s3 frame=dhritarashtra light=dhritarashtra}

> I now abandon Duryodhana for ever. {#q2 5.58}
> — Dhritarashtra

He grieved, he told the kings, for all of them who would follow that fool to Yama's house. When they saw the elephants lying like riven hills, and the Kuru host levelled by Bhima *like a large forest torn up by the roots*, they would remember his words. {#p4 5.58}
