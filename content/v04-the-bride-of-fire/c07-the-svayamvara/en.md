::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,bhima,arjuna,nakula,sahadeva,kunti light=yudhishthira}

On the road the Pandavas fell in with a crowd of young Brahmanas, also bound for Panchala, who asked where they were going. *We are uterine brothers,* [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) said, *travelling with our mother from Ekachakra.* {#p1 1.186}

The Brahmanas told them to come along. A great svayamvara was being held at Drupada's court, where the king's daughter would choose a husband from among the princes, and there would be feasting and gifts for everyone. They described her: lotus-eyed, faultless, with the fragrance of a blue lotus about her, the sister of Dhrishtadyumna who was born from the fire. {#p2 1.186}

They looked the Pandavas over and added, cheerfully, that the princess might just choose one of these handsome young men; and the big one might win some money wrestling. {#p3 1.186}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=vyasa,yudhishthira,kunti light=yudhishthira}

On the way they met [Vyasa](vyasa) again, and went on at his bidding, slowly, through woods and past lakes, studying as they went. In Drupada's capital they lodged in the house of a potter and lived by begging, as Brahmanas, and no one recognised them. {#p4 1.187}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=drupada,arjuna light=drupada}

[Drupada](drupada) had a secret. He had always wanted to give his daughter to Arjuna, the son of Pandu, though he never said so to anyone. So he had a bow made so stiff that no one but Arjuna, he thought, could bend it. High up, he set a mark above a revolving machine. {#p5 1.187}

> He that will string this bow and with these well-adorned arrows shoot the mark above the machine shall obtain my daughter. {#q1 1.187}
> — Drupada

***

::scene{#s4 frame=duryodhana,karna,drupada light=duryodhana,karna}

Kings came from every land. So did sages, and Brahmanas from every country, and [Duryodhana](duryodhana) with the Kurus and [Karna](karna). The amphitheatre stood on a level plain north-east of the city, walled and moated, under a canopy of many colours, scented with aloes and sandal, ringed by seven-storied white palaces like the peaks of Kailasa, where the visiting kings lodged. The citizens sat roaring like the sea on the platforms around it. {#p6 1.187}

The Pandavas went in too, and sat among the Brahmanas. For days there were actors and dancers and gifts; and on the sixteenth day, when the crowd was at its greatest, the princess came. {#p7 1.187}

***

::scene{#s5 frame=draupadi,dhrishtadyumna light=draupadi,dhrishtadyumna}

[Draupadi](draupadi) entered freshly bathed, richly dressed, carrying a golden dish of offerings and a garland. The priest lit the fire and poured the butter, and the music stopped. In the silence, [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna) took his sister by the arm and stood in the middle of the arena. {#p8 1.187}

> Hear ye assembled kings, this is the bow, that is the mark, and these are the arrows. Shoot the mark through the orifice of the machine with these five sharpened arrows. {#q2 1.187}
> — Dhrishtadyumna

Whoever did it, he said, being of good lineage, beauty and strength, would have his sister Krishna as his wife. {#p9 1.187}

***

::scene{#s6 frame=dhrishtadyumna,draupadi,duryodhana,duhshasana,karna,shakuni,ashwatthama,shalya,jarasandha,shishupala,balarama,krishna,somadatta,bhurishravas light=dhrishtadyumna,draupadi}

Then he named the suitors for her, one by one. {#p10 1.188}

[Duryodhana](duryodhana), [Duhshasana](duhshasana) and many other sons of Dhritarashtra, with Karna. [Shakuni](shakuni) and his brothers, the princes of Gandhara. [Ashwatthama](ashwatthama). Virata with his sons. [Jarasandha](jarasandha). Bhagadatta. [Shalya](shalya), king of Madra, with his son. [Somadatta](somadatta) of the Kurus, with his sons, among them [Bhurishravas](bhurishravas). Jayadratha of Sindhu. [Shishupala](shishupala) of Chedi. And of the Vrishnis, [Balarama](balarama) and [Krishna](krishna), with many of their kinsmen. {#p11 1.188}

These and many more, he told her, had come for her. *Thou shalt choose him for thy husband who amongst these will shoot the mark.* {#p12 1.188}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=1.188}
**A roll-call of the war.** Dhrishtadyumna's list runs to dozens of names. Read now, it is a list of nearly everyone who will fight at Kurukshetra. Many of the men who sit together in this amphitheatre will one day kill each other.
::
