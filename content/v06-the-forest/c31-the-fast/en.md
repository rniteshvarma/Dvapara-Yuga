::scene{#s1 frame=duryodhana,karna light=duryodhana}

[Duryodhana](duryodhana) camped on the way home, on a bed bright as fire, *looking like the moon under an eclipse*. Before dawn [Karna](karna) came to him, glad. By good luck the king was alive, he said, and had beaten the Gandharvas; Karna himself had fled. {#p1 3.245}

Duryodhana answered with tears in his voice. You do not know what happened, he said. The Gandharvas took us through the sky in chains, and our soldiers ran to the Pandavas, and the Pandavas set us free, and the Gandharva king told them why we had come. {#p2 3.246 3.247}

> I desired the earth to yield me a crevice, so that I might disappear there and then. {#q1 3.247}
> — Duryodhana

He would rather have died in the battle. Let them all go home. He would stay here and starve. {#p3 3.247}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=duryodhana,duhshasana light=duhshasana}

He called [Duhshasana](duhshasana) and gave him the kingdom. Rule the earth with Karna and Shakuni, he said, and look after our brothers; and he clasped his neck. *Go!* {#p4 3.247}

Duhshasana fell at his feet, weeping. {#p5 3.247}

> The earth may split, the vault of heaven may break in pieces, the sun may cast off his splendour, the moon may abandon his coolness, the wind may forsake its speed, the Himavat may be moved from its site, the waters of the ocean may dry up, and fire may abandon its heat, yet I, O king, may never rule the earth without thee. {#q2 3.247}
> — Duhshasana

***

::scene{#s3 frame=karna,shakuni,duryodhana light=karna}

Karna told him he was behaving like a child. Subjects ought to serve their king, he said; the Pandavas lived in his realm and had only done their duty. If Duryodhana fasted, Karna would sit at his feet and die too. *Thou wilt simply be an object of laughter with other kings.* {#p6 3.247 3.248}

[Shakuni](shakuni) said something stranger. Be grateful to the Pandavas, he said. Give them back their kingdom, make friends with them, and be happy. {#p7 3.249}

Duryodhana raised Duhshasana and smelt his head, and felt more ashamed than ever. He had done with virtue, wealth, friendship and kingship, he said. He spread kusha grass on the ground, put on rags, and sat down in silence to die. {#p8 3.249}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=duryodhana light=duryodhana}

The demons who had been beaten by the gods long ago, and lived in the underworld, knew that if Duryodhana died their side would be weakened. So they performed a rite, and a goddess rose from the fire and carried him down to them in the night. {#p9 3.249}

You are not a man, they told him, but our creation. {#p10 3.250}

> The upper part of thy body is wholly made of an assemblage of Vajras, and is, therefore, invulnerable to weapons of every description, O sinless one. The lower part of thy body, capable of captivating the female heart by its comeliness was made of flowers by the goddess herself—the wife of Mahadeva. {#q3 3.250}
> — the Danavas

Demons were already born on earth to fight for him, they said, and others would enter Bhishma, Drona and Karna and harden their hearts, so that they would kill without sparing sons, fathers or teachers. As for Arjuna: the soul of the demon Naraka had entered Karna, who would face Arjuna and Krishna. Indra knew it, and would come in disguise to take Karna's earrings and armour. But the Samsaptakas, thousands of demons born as warriors, would kill Arjuna instead. {#p11 3.250}

***

::scene{#s5 frame=karna,duryodhana light=karna}

The goddess carried him back, and Duryodhana thought it had all been a dream. But he told no one, and he got up believing he would beat the Pandavas. {#p12 3.250}

At dawn Karna came to him with joined hands. *No dead man conquereth his foes.* {#p13 3.250}

> I truly promise unto thee that I will slay Arjuna in battle. {#q4 3.250}
> — Karna

So Duryodhana rose and led his army home to Hastinapura. {#p14 3.250}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=3.250}
**A body of thunderbolts and flowers.** The demons say Duryodhana's upper body is made of thunderbolts and cannot be pierced, and his lower body of flowers, made by Shiva's wife. The text says no more here. Remember it at the end of the war.
::
