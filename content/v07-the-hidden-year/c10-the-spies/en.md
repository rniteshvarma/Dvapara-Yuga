::scene{#s1 frame=duryodhana,drona,karna,kripa,bhishma,duhshasana light=duryodhana}

Word spread from province to province: the king's cook Vallava and the general Kichaka had both been mighty men, but Kichaka had wronged other men's wives, and the Gandharvas had killed him. {#p1 4.25}

Meanwhile [Duryodhana](duryodhana)'s spies came home. They had searched forests and mountain tops, kingdoms and towns and camps, and found no trace. They had followed the Pandavas' charioteers to Dvaravati, but the Pandavas were not with them. *Salutations to thee, they are gone for good.* Only one piece of news would please him: Kichaka, who had beaten the Trigartas again and again, lay dead with all his brothers, killed at night by invisible Gandharvas. {#p2 4.25}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=duryodhana,karna,duhshasana light=duryodhana}

Duryodhana thought for a while. Most of the thirteenth year was gone. If the Pandavas got through the rest unseen, they would come back *like snakes of virulent poison*. They must be found now, and sent back to the forest. {#p3 4.26}

[Karna](karna) wanted more spies, better ones, well disguised, to search assemblies, palaces, shrines, mines, river banks, hermitages and mountain caves. [Duhshasana](duhshasana) agreed, though he thought they would never be found: perhaps they had crossed the ocean, or been eaten by wild beasts. {#p4 4.26}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=drona,bhishma light=bhishma}

[Drona](drona) said that men like the Pandavas do not perish. They were waiting for their moment. Send Brahmanas, Charanas and ascetics who knew them, he said, and search once more. {#p5 4.27}

[Bhishma](bhishma) agreed with Drona that they were alive, but he would not counsel finding them by spies. Instead he described the country where Yudhishthira must be living. {#p6 4.28}

> The ruler, O child, of the city or the province where king Yudhishthira resides cannot have any misfortune. {#q1 4.28}
> — Bhishma

There, the people would be generous and truthful, free of envy; the clouds would rain in season, the grain would be full, the fruit juicy, the garlands fragrant; the cattle would be many and none of them lean. Such a man, he said, could not be found out now, not even by Brahmanas. {#p7 4.28}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=kripa,duryodhana light=kripa}

[Kripa](kripa) called Bhishma's words wise, but said the spies should still find their track. The Pandavas would surely return when their term was over. Duryodhana should use the time to count his strength and his allies, fill his treasury and enlarge his army, so that he could either fight them or make a treaty on good terms. {#p8 4.29}

> O child, he that is solicitous of his welfare should not disregard even an ordinary foe. {#q2 4.29}
> — Kripa
