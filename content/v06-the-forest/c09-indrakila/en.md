::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,arjuna light=yudhishthira,arjuna}

Some time later, [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) took [Arjuna](arjuna) aside, held his hands, and spoke to him smiling. All the science of arms was with Bhishma, Drona, Kripa, Karna and Ashwatthama, and all of them were Duryodhana's. The whole earth was his. Arjuna was their only refuge. {#p1 3.37}

He gave Arjuna the knowledge Vyasa had given him. Go north, he said, armed, and do austerities, and see Indra; the gods gave all their weapons to Indra in their fear of Vritra, and he will give them to you. Go today. {#p2 3.37}

Arjuna took up the Gandiva and his quivers, put on his armour and his lizard-skin finger-guards, made offerings to the fire, and was ready. {#p3 3.37}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=draupadi,arjuna light=draupadi}

[Draupadi](draupadi) blessed him. {#p4 3.37}

> Let no one amongst us be ever again born in the order of Kshatriyas. {#q1 3.37}
> — Draupadi

Duryodhana had called her a cow in the assembly, she said, and many other hard things. {#p5 3.37}

> But the grief I experience at parting with thee is far greater than any I felt at those insults. {#q2 3.37}
> — Draupadi

Their lives and deaths, their kingdom, all depended on him. She bowed to every god she could name and asked them to protect him. Then Arjuna walked around his brothers and Dhaumya, and left. {#p6 3.37}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,indra light=arjuna,indra}

He crossed the Himalaya and the Gandhamadana in a single day, walking night and day without tiring, and came to Indrakila. There a voice from the sky said, *Stop!* Under a tree sat a thin, tawny ascetic with matted hair. {#p7 3.37}

There was no need of weapons here, the ascetic said, smiling: this was a place of peace. Throw away your bow. Arjuna would not. Then the ascetic said he was [Indra](indra), Arjuna's father. *Ask thou the boon thou desirest.* Arjuna asked for weapons. Why weapons, said Indra, when you have come this far? Ask for the worlds of bliss. {#p8 3.37}

> I desire not regions of bliss, nor objects of enjoyment, nor the state of a celestial. {#q3 3.37}
> — Arjuna

He had left his brothers in the forest with their enemy unpunished. Indra was pleased. See Shiva, the three-eyed god, he said, and then I will give you every weapon. And he vanished. {#p9 3.37}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=arjuna,shiva light=arjuna}

So Arjuna went on alone into the forest on the breast of the Himalaya, dressed in grass and a black deer-skin. The first month he ate fruit every third night; the second, every sixth; the third, once a fortnight. In the fourth month he lived on air, standing on his toes with his arms raised, leaning on nothing. {#p10 3.38}

The earth around him began to smoke. The sages went to [Shiva](shiva) and asked him to stop it. Shiva told them not to worry. {#p11 3.38}

> His wish is not for heaven, nor for prosperity, nor for long life. {#q4 3.38}
> — Shiva

*And I will accomplish, even, this day, all that is desired by him.* {#p12 3.38}
