::scene{#s1 frame=arjuna,bhima,jayadratha light=arjuna}

[Arjuna](arjuna) shot Jayadratha's horses from two miles away. Jayadratha jumped down and ran. *Possessed of so little manliness*, Arjuna called after him, *how couldst thou dare to take away a lady by force?* {#p1 3.269}

[Bhima](bhima) caught him by the hair, lifted him up and dashed him to the ground, and kicked him, and knelt on him and struck him until he fainted. Arjuna reminded him of their sister. {#p2 3.270}

> This sinful wretch hath done a cruel injury to Krishna, who never can bear such treatment. He, therefore, deserveth to die at hands! But what can I do? {#q1 3.270}
> — Bhima

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhima,jayadratha light=bhima}

So Bhima took a crescent-headed arrow and shaved Jayadratha's head, leaving five tufts of hair. {#p3 3.270}

*If thou wishest to live*, he said, *in public assemblies and in open courts thou must say,—I am the slave of the Pandavas.* Jayadratha, trembling and covered in dust, said, *Be it so!* They chained him and threw him into a chariot and brought him to Yudhishthira. {#p4 3.270}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=yudhishthira,draupadi,jayadratha light=yudhishthira}

Yudhishthira smiled and told them to let him go. *Tell Draupadi*, said Bhima, *that this wretch hath become the slave of the Pandavas.* {#p5 3.270}

> Let him off! He hath become a slave of the king's and thou, too, hast disfigured him by leaving five tufts of hair on his head. {#q2 3.270}
> — Draupadi

Jayadratha bowed to Yudhishthira. *Thou art a free man now; I emancipate thee!* said Yudhishthira. *Now go away and be careful not to do such thing again; shame to thee!* {#p6 3.270}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=jayadratha,shiva,arjuna light=jayadratha}

Jayadratha went away full of shame and grief, but not home. He went to the source of the Ganga and did fierce penance to Shiva, and Shiva came to him and offered a boon. He asked to be able to defeat all five Pandavas in battle. {#p7 3.270}

That cannot be, said Shiva. No one can kill or conquer them. {#p8 3.270}

> Save Arjuna, however, thou shall be able to only check them (once) on the field of battle! {#q3 3.270}
> — Shiva

Arjuna was Nara, Shiva said, the friend of Narayana, and Shiva himself had given him the Pashupata. *Be it so*, said Jayadratha, and went home. {#p9 3.270}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=3.270}
**One day.** Remember Shiva's boon: for one day, Jayadratha will be able to hold back every Pandava except Arjuna. The epic is setting up a day of the war, years before it comes.
::
