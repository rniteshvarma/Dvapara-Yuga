::scene{#s1 frame=susharma,virata light=susharma}

Night and dust stopped the fighting for a while. Then the moon rose, and it began again. [Susharma](susharma) and his younger brother came down from their chariots, maces in hand, and broke the Matsya army. They killed [Virata](virata)'s horses, his charioteer and the guards behind him, took the old king alive, put him on Susharma's own chariot and drove off the field. The Matsyas fled in every direction. {#p1 4.33}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=yudhishthira,bhima light=yudhishthira}

[Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) turned to [Bhima](bhima). They had lived well in Virata's city; now they must repay the debt. {#p2 4.33}

> The king of the Matsyas hath been taken by the Trigartas. Do thou, O mighty-armed one, rescue him, so that he may not fall under the power of the enemy. {#q1 4.33}
> — Yudhishthira

Bhima looked at a great tree. He would pull it up and use it as a mace, he said, and they could all stand back and watch. {#p3 4.33}

> Do not, O Bhima, commit such a rash act. Let the tree stand there. {#q2 4.33}
> — Yudhishthira

If he fought like that, people would say *This is Bhima*. Let him take a human weapon, a bow or a sword or an axe, and the twins would guard his wheels. {#p4 4.33}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhima,susharma,virata,yudhishthira,nakula,sahadeva light=bhima}

So Bhima took up a bow and poured arrows like a rain-cloud, and ran at Susharma calling *Stay! Stay!* Susharma turned back with his brothers to fight, and Bhima smashed the chariots that came against him and went through the infantry with a mace. Yudhishthira killed a thousand, Bhima seven thousand, Nakula seven hundred and Sahadeva three hundred. {#p5 4.33}

Bhima killed Susharma's horses and pulled his charioteer to the ground. Virata leapt down from Susharma's chariot, took the Trigarta's mace, and ran after him, old as he was, *even like a lusty youth*. Susharma fled, until Bhima shamed him into turning. Then Bhima jumped from his chariot, ran at him *like a lion rushing at a small deer*, seized him by the hair, lifted him and dashed him to the ground, kicked his head and knelt on his chest until he lay senseless. The Trigarta army broke and fled, and the cattle were recovered. {#p6 4.33}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=bhima,susharma,yudhishthira,virata light=yudhishthira}

> This wretch given to wicked deeds doth not deserve to escape me with life. But what can I do? The king is so lenient! {#q3 4.33}
> — Bhima

He tied Susharma up, threw him on his chariot, and brought him to Yudhishthira. Yudhishthira smiled and told him to let the man go. Bhima set a condition first: Susharma must say in every court and assembly, *I am a slave*. {#p7 4.33}

> If thou regardest us as an authority, liberate this wicked wight. He hath already become king Virata's slave. {#q4 4.33}
> — Yudhishthira

> Thou art freed. Go thou a free man, and never act again in this way. {#q5 4.33}
> — Yudhishthira, to Susharma

Susharma hung his head in shame, bowed to Virata, and went. {#p8 4.34}

***

::scene{#s5 frame=virata,yudhishthira light=virata}

That night, on the battlefield, Virata heaped wealth and honour on his four servants. *All these gems of mine are now as much mine as yours.* He would make Kanka king of the Matsyas; he owed him his sons and his kingdom. {#p9 4.34}

Yudhishthira thanked him, and asked only that messengers be sent to the city with the news of victory. They reached the city that night, and at sunrise proclaimed the king's victory at the gates. {#p10 4.34}
