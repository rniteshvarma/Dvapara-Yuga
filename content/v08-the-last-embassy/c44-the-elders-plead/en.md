::scene{#s1 frame=bhishma,duryodhana light=bhishma}

[Bhishma](bhishma) spoke next. Krishna had spoken for peace between kinsmen; if [Duryodhana](duryodhana) did not follow him, he would know neither prosperity nor happiness. He would destroy the glory of the Bharatas while his own father still lived, and lose his life with all his brothers and sons. {#p1 5.125}

> Be not the exterminator of thy race, be not a wicked man, let not thy heart be sinful, do not tread the path of unrighteousness. Do not sink thy father and mother into an ocean of grief. {#q1 5.125}
> — Bhishma

***

::scene{#s2 frame=drona,vidura,duryodhana light=drona}

Then [Drona](drona), while Duryodhana sat breathing hard with rage. Those who egged him on could not give him victory; when the fighting came they would put the weight on other men's necks. The army with Vasudeva and Arjuna in it could not be beaten. He had said everything there was to say, Drona ended; let him do as he liked. {#p2 5.125}

[Vidura](vidura) looked at Duryodhana and said he did not grieve for him. He grieved for the old couple. {#p3 5.125}

> I grieve, however, for this old couple, viz., Gandhari and thy father. {#q2 5.125}
> — Vidura

They would lose their protector, and wander the earth like a pair of birds with their wings cut off, living on alms. {#p4 5.125}

Then [Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra) himself, before all the kings: let him accept Krishna's words. The time had come; let him not let it pass. {#p5 5.125}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhishma,drona,bhima,yudhishthira light=bhima}

Bhishma and Drona spoke together once more, and every sentence ended the same way. While the two Krishnas had not yet put on their armour, while Gandiva still lay idle, while Dhaumya had not yet poured libations into the fire of war, *let hostility cease*. {#p6 5.126}

> As yet Bhima doth not, with his hero-slaying mace, make the heads of warriors fighting from the backs of elephants roll on the field of battle, like the palmyra-fruits in the season of their ripening, so let hostility cease. {#q3 5.126}
> — Bhishma and Drona

Let [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) embrace him as he bowed, and lay on his shoulder the right hand marked with the banner and the hook. Let Bhima embrace him, and the three younger ones bow to him, and let him smell their heads. Let the kings weep for joy to see it, and the news go out to every city. {#p7 5.126}
