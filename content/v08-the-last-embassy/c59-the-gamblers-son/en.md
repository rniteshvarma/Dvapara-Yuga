::scene{#s1 frame=uluka,yudhishthira light=uluka}

[Uluka](uluka) came into the Pandava camp and spoke first to [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira). He knew what envoys were, Uluka said; let him not be angry if Uluka repeated only what he had been told. *You have no fear*, Yudhishthira answered; say it. And before Krishna, Drupada and his sons, Virata and all the kings, Uluka said it. {#p1 5.162}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=5.162}
The text gives Duryodhana's message twice, once as his instructions to Uluka and again as Uluka delivers it, and Uluka then repeats it a third time to Arjuna. It is told once here, in the previous chapter.
::

> Like a frog within a well, why dost thou not realise the strength of this assembled host of monarchs, which resembleth the very celestial host, and which is protected by these kings like the gods protecting theirs in heaven. {#q1 5.162}
> — Uluka

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhima,krishna,uluka light=bhima}

The Pandavas had been angry enough the first time. Now they stood up and stretched their arms, looking at one another like enraged snakes. [Bhima](bhima) breathed hard and looked sideways at Krishna with bloodshot eyes. Krishna smiled at Uluka: go at once, and tell Duryodhana his words had been heard and understood, and let it be as he wished. {#p2 5.163}

But Uluka said it all again to [Arjuna](arjuna), *like one annoying wrathful snake of virulent poison by means of a stake*. Arjuna wiped the sweat from his forehead. Dhrishtadyumna, Shikhandi, Satyaki, the Kekayas, Ghatotkacha, the sons of Draupadi, Abhimanyu and the twins leapt up from their seats. {#p3 5.163}

Bhima, gnashing his teeth, told Uluka they had borne it all only for their elder brother's sake. The ocean might break its shores and the mountains split, but he would drink Duhshasana's blood, and send to Yama anyone who came at him, even with Bhishma at their head. [Sahadeva](sahadeva) said he would kill Uluka first, before Shakuni's eyes, and then Shakuni. {#p4 5.163}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,uluka light=arjuna}

Arjuna smiled at Bhima. Uluka did not deserve harsh words; what fault does an envoy commit, saying only what he was told? Then he gave his answer for Duryodhana. {#p5 5.163}

> When the morrow cometh, stationed at the head of my division, the answer to these words shall I give through Gandiva! For they that are eunuchs, answer in words! {#q2 5.163}
> — Arjuna

The kings applauded. Yudhishthira, red-eyed and sighing like a snake, sent his own word: a man who calls his foes to battle relying on the strength of others is no Kshatriya. Let Duryodhana fight on his own strength, not put in front of him men the Pandavas revered. {#p6 5.163}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=krishna,uluka,arjuna light=krishna}

[Krishna](krishna) had a message too. Did Duryodhana think he would not fight because he had agreed only to drive Arjuna's chariot? If his anger was roused he could burn all those kings like straw; but at Yudhishthira's command he would only hold the reins. {#p7 5.163}

> If thou fliest beyond the limits of the three worlds, if thou sinkest into the depths of the earth, thou shalt, even at these places, behold Arjuna's car tomorrow morning. {#q3 5.163}
> — Krishna

Bhima's words were not empty, Krishna said: know that Duhshasana's blood had already been drunk. {#p8 5.163}
