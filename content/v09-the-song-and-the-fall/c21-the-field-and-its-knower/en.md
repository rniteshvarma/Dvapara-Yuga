This body, [Krishna](krishna) told [Arjuna](arjuna), is called the *field*, and the one who knows it the *knower of the field*; and he himself is the knower in every field. True knowledge is knowledge of the field and of its knower. {#p1 6.37}

The field is the great elements, the sense of *I*, understanding, the unmanifest, the senses and the mind and their objects, desire and aversion, pleasure and pain, the body, consciousness and courage. And knowledge is humility, sincerity, harmlessness, patience, uprightness, service of the teacher, purity, steadiness, self-control, indifference to the objects of sense, seeing the evil of birth, death, age and disease, freedom from clinging to son, wife and home, evenness in good and bad fortune, single devotion, love of solitude. All that is contrary is ignorance. {#p2 6.37}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna light=krishna}

What is to be known is the supreme Brahman, without beginning, neither being nor not-being: with hands and feet, eyes, heads and faces on every side, pervading everything; shining with all the senses yet without senses; unattached yet supporting all; outside and inside all beings, moving and unmoving; far away and near; undivided, yet seeming divided; the light of lights, beyond darkness, seated in every heart. {#p3 6.37}

Nature and Spirit are both without beginning. Nature is the cause of action; Spirit, dwelling in nature, enjoys the qualities born of it, and its tie to them is the cause of its births in good and evil wombs. {#p4 6.37}

> He seeth the Supreme Lord dwelling alike in all beings, the Imperishable in the Perishable. {#q1 6.37}
> — Krishna

He who sees that all actions are done by nature alone, and that the self is not the doer, sees truly. As space, everywhere present, is never stained because it is so subtle, so the self in every body is never stained. {#p5 6.37}

> As the single Sun lights up the entire world, so the Spirit, O Bharata, lights up the entire (sphere of) matters. {#q2 6.37}
> — Krishna
