::scene{#s1 frame=drona,arjuna light=drona}

[Arjuna](arjuna) came on with the rattle of his chariot and the twang of the Gandiva, and [Drona](drona) knew the banner-top at a distance and the ape roaring on it. Then two arrows fell together at his feet, and two more passed by, barely touching his ears. {#p1 4.53}

> Completing the period of exile and having achieved many wonderful feats, Partha saluteth me and whispereth in my ears. {#q1 4.53}
> — Drona

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,uttar,kripa,duryodhana light=arjuna}

Arjuna looked for one face in the army. There stood Drona, and his son, and Bhishma, Kripa and Karna. But the king was not there. *I suspect that anxious to save his life, he retreateth by the southern road, taking away with him the kine.* He told [Uttara](uttar) to leave the chariot-warriors and drive to wherever Duryodhana was: beat him, and the rest would count themselves beaten. {#p2 4.53}

[Kripa](kripa) guessed what he meant to do. {#p3 4.53}

> Of what use to us would the kine be or this vast wealth also, if Duryodhana were to sink, like a boat, in the ocean of Partha? {#q2 4.53}
> — Kripa

Arjuna announced his name and covered that division with arrows *thick as locusts*, until no one could see earth or sky. He blew his conch, twanged his bow, and made the creatures on his banner roar louder. And at that noise the cattle raised their tails, lowed together, turned round, and ran back along the southern road towards the city of the Matsyas. {#p4 4.53}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,vikarna,karna,uttar light=arjuna}

The Kuru leaders saw the cattle running home and fell upon Arjuna together. He told Uttara to take him to Karna. *Like an elephant desiring an encounter with another, the Suta's son of wicked soul eagerly desireth a battle with me.* {#p5 4.54}

[Vikarna](vikarna) came at him first; Arjuna cut his bow and his flagstaff, and Vikarna fled. Shatruntapa came next, and fell dead *like a tree from a mountain-top torn up by the wind*. Then Arjuna killed the red horses of Sangramajit, Karna's brother, and cut off his head with a crescent arrow. {#p6 4.54}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=karna,arjuna light=karna}

[Karna](karna) rushed at him like an elephant with tusks outstretched, and the Kauravas stood back to watch. He pierced Arjuna with twelve arrows, the horses, and Uttara's hand. Arjuna buried him, his car and his driver, in arrows; Karna burst out of them *like a blazing fire*, and the Kurus clapped and blew their conchs. They fought like the sun and moon behind clouds. Karna struck the four horses, the driver and the flagstaff. Then Arjuna drew to his ear and pierced Karna's arms and thighs, his head, forehead and neck. {#p7 4.54}

> And mangled and afflicted by the arrows shot by Partha the son of Pandu, Vikartana's son, quitted the van of battle, and quickly took to flight, like one elephant vanquished by another. {#q3 4.54}
