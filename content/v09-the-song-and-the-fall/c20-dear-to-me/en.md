Which worshippers know yoga best, [Arjuna](arjuna) asked: those who are always devoted to you, or those who meditate on the imperishable and unmanifest? {#p1 6.36}

Those who fix their minds on me and worship me with the highest faith, [Krishna](krishna) said, are the most devoted. Those who worship the imperishable, unmanifest, all-pervading, unthinkable, also reach him, with their senses held and their minds even, doing good to all creatures; but their path is harder, for the unmanifest is hard for embodied beings to reach. Those who give all their work to Krishna and meditate on him alone he lifts quickly out of the ocean of death. {#p2 6.36}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,arjuna light=krishna}

So fix your mind on me, Krishna said. If you cannot, seek me by steady practice. If you cannot do that, make my work your highest aim. If even that is too much, give up the fruit of every action. Knowledge is better than practice, meditation better than knowledge, giving up the fruit better than meditation, and from giving up comes peace at once. {#p3 6.36}

> He who hath no hatred for any creature, who is friendly and compassionate also, who is free from egoism, who hath no vanity, attachment, who is alike in pleasure and pain, who is forgiving, contented, always devoted. {#q1 6.36}
> — Krishna

> He through whom the world is not troubled, (and) who is not troubled by the world, who is free from joy, wrath, fear and anxieties, even he is dear to me. {#q2 6.36}
> — Krishna

The same to friend and foe, in honour and dishonour, in cold and heat, in praise and blame, silent, content with whatever comes, homeless, steady and full of faith: that man is dear to him. And those who follow this immortal way with faith, holding him as their highest goal, are dearest of all. {#p4 6.36}
