> Fear not, O king. Nor shouldest thou grieve for us. {#q1 5.55}
> — Duryodhana

[Duryodhana](duryodhana) told his father how, when the Pandavas first went into exile and Krishna and the kings came to them, he had been afraid, and Bhishma, Drona, Kripa and Ashwatthama had told him not to fear. Now the earth was his, the kings would walk into fire or sea for him, and Yudhishthira, frightened, was asking for only five villages. {#p1 5.55}

As for Bhima: {#p2 5.55}

> There is none on earth equal to me in an encounter with the mace. {#q2 5.55}
> — Duryodhana

He had learned from Balarama, who believed it. One blow and Bhima would go to Yama. Bhishma could die only when he chose; Drona was born in a pot, Kripa in a clump of reeds; Karna had Indra's infallible dart, given in exchange for his earrings. He had eleven akshauhinis against seven. How could he lose? {#p3 5.55}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=sanjaya,arjuna,hanuman light=arjuna}

What was Yudhishthira doing? he asked Sanjaya. Cheerful, said [Sanjaya](sanjaya), and so were Bhima and Arjuna. Arjuna had yoked his chariot and told him, *Behold, O Sanjaya, these preliminary signs. We will certainly conquer.* {#p4 5.56}

Its banner, made by the celestial artificer with Indra and Dhatri, spread a yojana every way and could not be blocked even by trees, its shapes always changing like a rainbow. Its hundred white horses, a gift of the Gandharva Chitrasena, were always made full again however many were killed. {#p5 5.56}

> And at Bhimasena's request, Hanumat, the son of the Wind-god, will also place his own image on it. {#q3 5.56}
> — Sanjaya

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=5.56}
This is the promise [Hanuman](hanuman) made to Bhima on the road to Kubera's lake, told in Volume VI, chapter 20.
::

***

::scene{#s3 frame=shikhandi,bhishma,shalya,yudhishthira,bhima,duryodhana,arjuna,karna,jayadratha,abhimanyu,dhrishtadyumna,drona,sahadeva,shakuni,nakula,uluka light=dhrishtadyumna}

Then Sanjaya told them how the Pandavas had already shared out their enemies, with [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna), who knew every battle array, as leader. [Bhishma](bhishma) was assigned to [Shikhandi](shikhandi). [Shalya](shalya) to [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira), though some thought them ill-matched. Duryodhana and his brothers to [Bhima](bhima). [Karna](karna) and [Jayadratha](jayadratha) to [Arjuna](arjuna). The sons of Duryodhana and Duhshasana to [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu). [Drona](drona) to Draupadi's sons, led by Dhrishtadyumna. [Shakuni](shakuni) to [Sahadeva](sahadeva), and the deceitful [Uluka](uluka) to [Nakula](nakula). {#p6 5.57}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=dhritarashtra,duryodhana light=dhritarashtra}

> Alas, all my foolish sons, addicted to deceitful dice, are already dead when it is the mighty Bhima with whom they desire to encounter in the field of battle. {#q4 5.57}
> — Dhritarashtra

Duryodhana answered that both sides were of one race and walked on one earth; why should victory go only to the Pandavas? His kings would catch them *like a herd of young deer by means of net*. {#p7 5.57}

> O Sanjaya, this my son speaketh like a mad man, for he is incapable of vanquishing in battle Yudhishthira the just. {#q5 5.57}
> — Dhritarashtra
