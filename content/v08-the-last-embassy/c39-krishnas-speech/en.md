[Krishna](krishna) spoke to [Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra), in a voice like rain-clouds that the whole hall could hear. {#p1 5.95}

> In order that, O Bharata, peace may be established between the Kurus and the Pandavas without a slaughter of the heroes, I have come hither. {#q1 5.95}
> — Krishna

The Kuru race was the noblest of all; it would be a pity if it did wrong, and a greater pity if the king did. His sons, led by Duryodhana, had abandoned virtue for greed. Peace was not hard. {#p2 5.95}

> The establishment of peace, O king, depends on thee and myself, O monarch. Set right thy sons, O thou of Kuru's race, and I will set the Pandavas right. {#q2 5.95}
> — Krishna

With the Pandavas beside Bhishma, Drona, Karna and the rest, who would dare fight him? He would rule the whole earth. But in war there would be only destruction; whoever fell, Pandava or Kaurava, what joy would he have? Let the kings go home embracing one another, eating and drinking together in their garlands. {#p3 5.95}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,yudhishthira light=krishna}

Then he gave the Pandavas' message. They had kept their pledge, twelve years in the forest and the thirteenth hidden, trusting their father to keep his. Let him now act as a father, and as a teacher to his pupils. And to the kings in the hall, they said this: {#p4 5.95}

> Where, in the presence of the virtuous members of an assembly, righteousness is sought to be overpowered by unrighteousness, and truth by the untruth, it is those members themselves that are vanquished and slain. {#q3 5.95}
> — the Pandavas

> Indeed, in that case, righteousness slayeth the members of that assembly, like a river eating away the roots of the trees on its bank. {#q4 5.95}
> — the Pandavas

[Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) had been sent to be burned, exiled, robbed by dice, had seen Draupadi dragged into the hall, and had still not swerved. The Pandavas were ready to serve the king, or to fight. Let him choose. {#p5 5.95}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=duryodhana light=duryodhana}

> All the rulers of earth there present highly applauded these words of Kesava within their hearts, but none of them ventured to say anything in the presence of Duryodhana. {#q5 5.95}
