::scene{#s1 frame=duryodhana,bhishma,dhritarashtra light=duryodhana}

Vidura was right, said [Duryodhana](duryodhana): Krishna could never be separated from the Pandavas. So give him nothing; with war decided, he would only think they honoured him out of fear. [Bhishma](bhishma) answered that Krishna was never angered by honour or its lack, and could not be thwarted; make peace through him. {#p1 5.88}

> I will imprison Janardana who is the refuge of the Pandavas. He will come here tomorrow morning; and when he is confined, the Vrishnis and the Pandavas, aye, the whole earth, will submit to me. {#q1 5.88}
> — Duryodhana

[Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra) and his counsellors were stricken. Krishna came as an envoy, a kinsman who had done them no wrong; this was not the custom of ages. {#p2 5.88}

> This wicked son of thine, O Dhritarashtra, hath his hour come. {#q2 5.88}
> — Bhishma

He would not listen to such words, Bhishma said, and rose in anger and left. {#p3 5.88}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,bhishma,drona,dhritarashtra light=krishna}

At dawn [Krishna](krishna) left Vrikasthala. All Dhritarashtra's sons but Duryodhana came out to meet him with Bhishma, [Drona](drona) and Kripa; the citizens lined the streets in thousands, bowing to the ground, and the great houses packed with women seemed ready to fall under their weight. His fast horses could only walk through the crowd. {#p4 5.89}

In Dhritarashtra's ash-coloured palace the blind king rose to greet him, with Drona, Bhishma, Kripa, Somadatta and Bahlika. Krishna honoured each by age, sat on a golden seat, received the cow, honey, curds and water, and laughed and joked with the Kurus as their kinship allowed. Then he went to Vidura's house. {#p5 5.89}

> — Vidura

And Krishna told him everything about the Pandavas. {#p6 5.89}
