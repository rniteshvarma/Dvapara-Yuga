::scene{#s1 frame=krishna,satyaki,garuda light=krishna}

The night passed. It was the month of Kartika, under the star Revati, the dewy season after autumn, with the fields full of crops. [Krishna](krishna) bathed, worshipped the Sun and Fire, touched a bull's tail, bowed to the Brahmanas, walked round the sacred fire, and told [Satyaki](satyaki) to load his conch, discus, mace and every weapon on the chariot. *Foes, however contemptible, should never be disregarded by even a powerful person.* {#p1 5.83}

His four horses, Shaibya, Sugriva, Meghapushpa and Valahaka, were yoked, and [Garuda](garuda) came and perched on his flagstaff. The sky cleared; birds circled to his right; the sacrificial fire burned smokeless; and the Rishis stood on his right as he set out for the Kurus. {#p2 5.83}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=yudhishthira,krishna,kunti,arjuna,bhima light=yudhishthira}

[Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) followed him a way, embraced him, and gave him a message for their mother. {#p3 5.83}

> Shall I, O Krishna, ever see the time when, O chastiser of foes, my afflictions being over, I shall be able to make my sorrowing mother happy? {#q1 5.83}
> — Yudhishthira

[Kunti](kunti) had run after them crying when they went into exile, and they had left her behind. Let Krishna salute her, embrace her, comfort her. Then [Arjuna](arjuna): if Duryodhana gave back what was theirs with honour, well; otherwise he would destroy the Kshatriya race. [Bhima](bhima), shaking with rage and joy, gave a roar that made horses and elephants foul themselves. {#p4 5.83}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,parashurama light=krishna}

On the road Krishna met Rishis blazing with Brahmic light, and got down to salute them. Jamadagni's son embraced him. The Rishis wanted to see all the kings of the earth gathered in the Kuru hall, and to hear what Krishna would say there. *We hope to see thee in the midst of the conclave, seated on an excellent seat mustering all thy energy and might.* {#p5 5.83}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=5.83}
"Jamadagni's son" is [Parashurama](parashurama), the same who taught Bhishma, Drona and Karna.
::

***

::scene{#s4 frame=krishna light=krishna}

As he went, the omens turned. There was thunder in a cloudless sky; rivers ran backwards; fires blazed up and the earth shook; a storm tore up trees by the thousand and battered Hastinapura. But wherever he passed, the winds were sweet, lotuses fell, the road was smooth and free of thorns, and women strewed flowers on him. {#p6 5.84}

At sunset he reached Vrikasthala, said his evening prayers, and ate with the village Brahmanas, and there he spent the night happily. {#p7 5.84}
