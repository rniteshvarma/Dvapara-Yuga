::scene{#s1 frame=arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

[Arjuna](arjuna) thought peace might still be possible, if properly offered. Krishna was the friend of both sides, as Prajapati is of the gods and the Asuras; whatever he chose, war or peace, they would honour. But wise counsel would be lost on Duryodhana *like seed sown in a barren soil*. {#p1 5.78}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,arjuna light=krishna}

[Krishna](krishna) agreed to try. But some things lay beyond human effort. {#p2 5.79}

> Behold, the soil is moistened and divested of weeds by human exertion. Without rain, however, O son of Kunti, is never yieldeth crops. {#q1 5.79}
> — Krishna

He would do all that human effort could do, but could not control what was destined. Duryodhana would never give up the kingdom without being destroyed. *That grave act also which was ordained in heaven is known to thee.* Do not expect peace with the foe. {#p3 5.79}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=nakula,sahadeva,satyaki light=sahadeva}

[Nakula](nakula) said opinions change with times: in the forest they had wanted one thing, now with seven akshauhinis, another. Let Krishna speak gently first, and then with threats. {#p4 5.80}

But [Sahadeva](sahadeva) wanted war. {#p5 5.81}

> Even if the Kauravas express their desire for peace with the Pandavas, still, O thou of Dasarha's race, provoke thou a war with them. {#q2 5.81}
> — Sahadeva

He had seen the princess of Panchala dragged into the hall; how could his anger be appeased without Duryodhana's death? [Satyaki](satyaki) agreed, and all the warriors there set up a lion's roar. *Excellent! Excellent!* {#p6 5.81}
