In the afternoon [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna) fought Ashwatthama, Shalya and Kripa, and killed Ashwatthama's famous horses; Ashwatthama climbed onto Shalya's chariot and fought on. [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu) came to help, and pierced Shalya, Kripa and Ashwatthama. {#p1 6.55}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=abhimanyu,lakshmana_k,duryodhana light=abhimanyu}

Then Duryodhana's son [Lakshmana](lakshmana_k) rushed at his cousin. He struck Abhimanyu, and Abhimanyu struck him with five hundred arrows; Lakshmana cut Abhimanyu's bow in the middle, and the people shouted. Abhimanyu took up a tougher bow, and the two young men went on piercing each other. Seeing his son hard pressed, [Duryodhana](duryodhana) came, and all the kings closed round Abhimanyu with their chariots; and he, *equal in prowess unto Krishna himself*, was not shaken at all. {#p2 6.55}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,krishna,bhishma,drona light=arjuna}

Then [Arjuna](arjuna) came to rescue his son, and Bhishma and Drona and the kings rushed at him. The dust hid the sky. Thousands of elephants and hundreds of kings came within reach of his arrows and could go no further. Sky, earth and sun disappeared under his shafts; chariot-warriors wandered without chariots, riders fled their horses and elephants, and he cut off the raised arms of men still holding maces and swords. No one in the Kuru army could stand against him. Arjuna and Krishna blew their conchs. {#p3 6.55}

[Bhishma](bhishma) smiled and turned to Drona. Arjuna with Krishna was dealing with their troops as only he could; today he looked like the Destroyer at the end of an age. {#p4 6.55}

> This vast host again (of ours) is incapable of being rallied. Behold, looking at one another, our troops are flying away. {#q1 6.55}
> — Bhishma

The sun was going down behind the western mountain, he said, and the tired, frightened men would not fight. So Bhishma withdrew the Kuru army, and the Pandavas withdrew theirs, and twilight fell on the second day. {#p5 6.55}
