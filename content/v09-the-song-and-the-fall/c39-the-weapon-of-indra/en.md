::scene{#s1 frame=arjuna,duryodhana,bhishma,shalya light=arjuna}

As Panchajanya sounded, the Kurus shouted, and [Duryodhana](duryodhana) came on with Bhishma and Bhurishravas *like a comet risen for consuming a constellation*. Bhurishravas hurled seven golden javelins, Duryodhana a lance, [Shalya](shalya) a mace, [Bhishma](bhishma) a dart; [Arjuna](arjuna) cut them all down in the air. {#p1 6.59}

Then he drew Gandiva with both hands, spoke the mantras, and called up the Mahendra weapon, the weapon of Indra, into the sky. From it fell showers of arrows like blazing fire, and they held the whole Kuru army. They cut off arms and bows and banners, went through kings and elephants and horses. The conchs, the drums and the chariots were all silenced by the twang of Gandiva; and hearing it, Virata and Drupada came gladly, and the Kurus stood frozen where they heard it, none daring to go toward it. {#p2 6.59}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

Arjuna made a river on the field: its water blood, its froth fat, its banks dead elephants and horses, its mud entrails and marrow, its floating moss the hair of human heads, with jackals, vultures and Rakshasas along its shores. To those still living it looked like the Vaitarani, the river of the dead. {#p3 6.59}

The Chedis, Panchalas and Matsyas roared their victory, and Arjuna and Krishna roared with them. Seeing the sun go red and that weapon spread over the field like the end of an age, Bhishma, Drona, Duryodhana and Bahlika withdrew their troops for the night, and Arjuna went back to camp with his brothers. {#p4 6.59}

***

::scene{#s3 light=duryodhana}

In the Kuru tents, lit by thousands of torches, the warriors talked into the dark. {#p5 6.59}

> In today's battle Arjuna hath slain ten thousand car-warriors, and full seven hundred elephants. {#q1 6.59}
> — the Kuru warriors

The westerners, the Sauviras, the Kshudrakas and the Malavas were all dead. Drona, Kripa, Jayadratha, Bahlika, Bhurishravas, Shalya and Bhishma himself had all been beaten together by Kiritin alone, *that one mighty car-warrior in the world*. And so ended the third day. {#p6 6.59}
