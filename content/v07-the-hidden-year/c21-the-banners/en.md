When Karna had fled, Duryodhana's warriors came at [Arjuna](arjuna) one division after another, and he stood against them *like the shore withstanding the fury of the surging sea*. There was no one, on chariot, horse or elephant, with two fingers' breadth of his body unwounded. Flags and umbrellas blew away on the wind; loosed horses ran dragging broken chariots. {#p1 4.55}

He would not kill men who could not defend themselves, so he fell back and struck the army from behind with arrows *like hawks let off by fowlers*. His bow was always drawn to a full circle. The dust and blood turned the sun red, so that the noon sky looked like evening. To the Kurus it seemed that Indra and all the gods were killing them, or that Death himself had taken Arjuna's form. {#p2 4.55}

He sent seventy-three arrows at Drona, ten at Duhsaha, eight at Ashwatthama, twelve at Duhshasana, three at Kripa, arrows at Bhishma, a hundred at Duryodhana; and Karna he pierced in the ear with a barbed shaft. {#p3 4.55}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,uttar,kripa,drona,ashwatthama,duryodhana,karna,bhishma light=uttar}

Where now? asked [Uttara](uttar). Arjuna pointed out the banners one by one. {#p4 4.55}

The warrior in tiger-skin mail, with a blue flag and red horses, was [Kripa](kripa); go there first. The flag with a golden water-pot was [Drona](drona)'s. {#p5 4.55}

> Do thou, therefore, circumambulate that great hero cheerfully. Let us bend our heads there, for that is the eternal virtue. If Drona strikes my body first, then I shall strike him, for then he will not be able to resent it. {#q1 4.55}
> — Arjuna

The flag with the device of a bow was [Ashwatthama](ashwatthama)'s. The golden elephant was [Duryodhana](duryodhana)'s, first of Drona's pupils in lightness of hand. The flag with the elephant's binding-rope was [Karna](karna)'s: be careful there. And the blue flag with five stars and a sun, under a pure white umbrella, with a golden helmet, was the grandsire's. {#p6 4.55}

> Let him be approached last of all, for he may, even now, be an obstacle to me. {#q2 4.55}
> — Arjuna, of Bhishma

***

::scene{#s3 frame=indra,arjuna light=indra}

The sky filled. [Indra](indra) came in his car with the Vishvas and Maruts, and three and thirty gods, Gandharvas, Nagas, Pitris and great Rishis came each in his own car to see their weapons used among men and to watch Bhishma and Arjuna meet. The kings of old were there, Shibi and Yayati and Nahusha and Raghu and Nala. The dust settled, a scented wind cooled the fighters, and the sky blazed with jewelled cars. {#p7 4.56}

> And the slayer of Vala, although he steadfastly gazed at his son on the field of battle, was not satiated with such gazing. {#q3 4.56}
