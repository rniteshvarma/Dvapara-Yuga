Now, [Krishna](krishna) said, hear how you may know me wholly. Among thousands of men hardly one strives for perfection, and among those who strive hardly one knows him as he is. {#p1 6.31}

Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, understanding and the sense of *I*: that is his lower nature, eightfold. His higher nature is the life by which the universe is held up. He is the source and the dissolution of everything. {#p2 6.31}

> Upon me is all this like a row of pearls on a string. {#q1 6.31}
> — Krishna

> I am the splendour of both the moon and the sun, I am the Om in all the Vedas, the sound in space, and the manliness in men. {#q2 6.31}
> — Krishna

He is the fragrance of the earth, the brightness of fire, the life in all beings, the austerity of ascetics, the eternal seed. {#p3 6.31}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,arjuna light=arjuna}

Everything made of the three qualities, goodness, passion and darkness, comes from him, but he is not in them; they are in him. Deluded by these qualities, the world does not know him, and his divine illusion is hard to cross; only those who come to him cross it. {#p4 6.31}

Four kinds of doers of good deeds worship him, the distressed among them. Of them the man of knowledge, always devoted to the One, is dearest, and is Krishna's very self. {#p5 6.31}

> At the end of many births, the man possessed of knowledge attaineth to me, (thinking) that Vasudeva is all this. {#q3 6.31}
> — Krishna

Such a great soul is very rare. Whatever form a man worships with faith, Krishna makes that faith firm, and he gets his wish from it; but those who worship the gods go to the gods, and those who worship Krishna come to him. Veiled in his illusion he is not seen by all. He knows all that was, is and will be, but no one knows him. {#p6 6.31}
