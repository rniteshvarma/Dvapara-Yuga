::scene{#s1 frame=arjuna,uluka,bhishma light=arjuna}

[Arjuna](arjuna) looked at [Uluka](uluka) with red eyes. Duryodhana had put the oldest of the Kshatriyas at the head of his army, believing the Pandavas would never kill the son of Ganga out of kindness. Then let him know this: Arjuna would kill [Bhishma](bhishma) first, before every bowman, and throw him from his chariot. {#p1 5.164}

Let Duryodhana not judge Arjuna by his own conduct. The difference between them was the difference between truth and falsehood. {#p2 5.164}

> I do not wish harm to even insects and ants. What shall I say, therefore, of my ever wishing harm to my kinsmen? {#q1 5.164}
> — Arjuna

That was why he had asked for only five villages. Arjuna did not make a vow twice, he said. It would all come true. {#p3 5.164}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhima,nakula,sahadeva,virata,drupada light=bhima}

[Bhima](bhima) spoke again. {#p4 5.164}

> Thou shalt have to dwell in the stomach of a vulture or in Hastinapura. {#q2 5.164}
> — Bhima

He would drink Duhshasana's blood, kill Duryodhana's brothers, break his thighs, and put his foot on Duryodhana's head in front of Yudhishthira. [Nakula](nakula) said only that he had heard and understood, and would do as he was bidden. [Sahadeva](sahadeva) said Duryodhana would repent, with his children and kin, of the joy he took in their suffering. Old [Virata](virata) and [Drupada](drupada) said: whether we are slaves or masters will be known tomorrow. {#p5 5.164}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=shikhandi,dhrishtadyumna,yudhishthira light=shikhandi}

> Without doubt, I have been created by the high-souled Creator for the destruction of Bhishma. I shall assuredly slay Bhishma in the sight of all bowmen. {#q3 5.164}
> — Shikhandi

[Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna) said he would kill Drona with all his followers, and do a deed no one else would ever do. And [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) spoke last, gently. He had never wanted his kinsmen killed; it was Duryodhana's fault, and he would have to let these men do their great deeds. {#p6 5.164}

> Go hence, O Uluka, without delay or stay here, O sire, for, blessed be thou, we too are thy kinsmen. {#q4 5.164}
> — Yudhishthira

Uluka went back and repeated it all faithfully. Duryodhana ordered the armies drawn up before sunrise, and riders on chariots, camels and fast horses carried Karna's order through the camp: *Array yourselves before sunrise tomorrow!* {#p7 5.164}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=dhrishtadyumna,arjuna,bhima,abhimanyu,drona light=dhrishtadyumna}

Dhrishtadyumna set each of his warriors against a man on the other side. Arjuna against Karna; Bhima against Duryodhana; Dhrishtaketu against Shalya; Nakula against Kritavarma; Satyaki against Jayadratha; Sahadeva against Shakuni; the sons of Draupadi against the Trigartas; Shikhandi in the van against Bhishma. [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu) he set against Karna's son Vrishasena and all the other kings, {#p8 5.165}

> for he regarded Abhimanyu as superior to Arjuna himself in battle. {#q5 5.165}

[Drona](drona) he kept for himself. Then, with a firm heart, he waited for the battle. {#p9 5.165}
