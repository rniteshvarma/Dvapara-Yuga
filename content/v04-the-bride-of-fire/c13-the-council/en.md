::scene{#s1 frame=duryodhana,duhshasana,karna,shakuni,ashwatthama,kripa light=duryodhana}

Spies carried the news to every king: the man who strung the bow was Arjuna, the man with the tree was Bhima, and the Pandavas, whom everyone thought burnt to death, were alive and married to Draupadi. The kings said: *fie on Bhishma, fie on Dhritarashtra!* {#p1 1.202}

[Duryodhana](duryodhana) rode home to Hastinapura with his brothers, Ashwatthama, Shakuni, Karna and Kripa, heavy-hearted. [Duhshasana](duhshasana) said softly that if Arjuna had not been disguised as a Brahmana he could never have won her. *Fie on our exertions, O brother! The Pandavas are still alive!* {#p2 1.202}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=vidura,dhritarashtra light=vidura,dhritarashtra}

[Vidura](vidura) heard and was glad. He went to [Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra) and said: *The Kurus are prospering by good luck!* {#p3 1.202}

The blind king thought he meant that Draupadi had chosen Duryodhana, and cried *What good luck, O Vidura!* and ordered ornaments made for the bride. Then Vidura told him: she had chosen the Pandavas, who were alive and well and honoured by Drupada, with his many allies and their armies. {#p4 1.202}

Dhritarashtra said at once that the sons of Pandu were as dear to him as they had been to Pandu, and dearer now; who would not want Drupada for an ally? *O king,* said Vidura, *let thy understanding remain so without change for a hundred years!* {#p5 1.202}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=dhritarashtra,duryodhana,karna light=dhritarashtra,duryodhana}

As soon as Vidura had gone, Duryodhana and Karna came in. They could not speak freely in front of Vidura, they said. Why was the king praising his enemies? {#p6 1.202}

> I desire to do exactly what you would recommend. But I do not wish to inform Vidura of it even by a change of muscle. {#q1 1.203}
> — Dhritarashtra

***

::scene{#s4 frame=duryodhana,bhima,draupadi,drupada light=duryodhana}

Duryodhana had a dozen schemes. Turn the sons of Kunti against the sons of Madri. Bribe Drupada and his ministers to abandon them. Tempt them to stay in Panchala for good. Set Draupadi against her husbands, or them against her. Send spies to kill Bhima, the strongest of them, without whom Arjuna was not worth a quarter of Karna. Tempt them with other women. Or bring them home and have them murdered there. Only it must be done soon, before they came to trust Drupada. {#p7 1.203}

***

::scene{#s5 frame=karna,duryodhana,krishna light=karna}

[Karna](karna) thought none of it would work. {#p8 1.204}

> It doth not seem to me, O Duryodhana, that thy reasoning is well-founded. O perpetuator of the Kuru race, no method will succeed against the Pandavas. {#q2 1.204}
> — Karna

Duryodhana had tried tricks when they were boys living under his roof, and failed. Now they were grown, and far away. They could not be divided, since they shared one wife; Draupadi had chosen them when they were poor, and would not leave them now; Drupada was honest and could not be bought. There was only one way: attack now, with the whole army, before Drupada was ready and before Krishna came with the Yadavas. *Vanquish them, therefore, by thy prowess.* {#p9 1.204}

Dhritarashtra praised him, and said Bhishma, Drona and Vidura must be called in too. {#p10 1.204}

***

::scene{#s6 frame=bhishma,dhritarashtra,duryodhana light=bhishma}

[Bhishma](bhishma) would not hear of a quarrel. Pandu was to him what Dhritarashtra was, and Kunti's sons what Gandhari's were. {#p11 1.205}

> Concluding a treaty with those heroes, let half the land be given unto them. This is without doubt, the paternal kingdom of those foremost ones of the Kuru race. {#q3 1.205}
> — Bhishma

Since the fire, he said, he had hardly been able to look anyone in the face; the world blamed Duryodhana more than Purochana. The Pandavas' return had washed that stain away. Let it stay washed. {#p12 1.205}

***

::scene{#s7 frame=drona,karna light=drona,karna}

[Drona](drona) agreed with Bhishma. Send a messenger to Drupada, he said, with rich gifts, and tell him how glad Dhritarashtra and Duryodhana are of the alliance; then invite the Pandavas home and give them their father's throne. {#p13 1.206}

Karna sneered. Bhishma and Drona had grown fat on the king's wealth and favour, and now advised against his interests; and anyway, everything was settled by destiny. {#p14 1.206}

> As thou art wicked it is evident thou sayest so in consequence of the wickedness of thy intent. {#q4 1.206}
> — Drona

If his advice was not taken, Drona said, the Kurus would soon be destroyed. {#p15 1.206}

***

::scene{#s8 frame=vidura,dhritarashtra,krishna,balarama,satyaki,drupada light=vidura}

Then Vidura spoke. Bhishma and Drona were the king's best friends, the wisest men he had, and they had never advised him wrongly. Could Arjuna be beaten by Indra himself? Bhima by the gods? With Balarama their ally, [Krishna](krishna) their counsellor, [Satyaki](satyaki) on their side and Drupada and his sons as kin, they had already won. {#p16 1.207}

> Know where Krishna is, all of them must be, and where Krishna is, there victory also must be! {#q5 1.207}
> — Vidura

Duryodhana, Karna and Shakuni were sinful, foolish and young, Vidura said; the king should not listen to them. {#p17 1.207}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=1.207,1.202}
**Two faces.** Dhritarashtra tells Vidura one thing and his son another, and admits it. The council that follows is the whole quarrel in miniature: Duryodhana's tricks, Karna's war, Bhishma's half the kingdom, Drona's diplomacy and Vidura's warning. Years later, before the war, each of them will say much the same again.
::
