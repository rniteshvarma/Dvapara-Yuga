::scene{#s1 frame=duryodhana,bhishma,drona,karna,kripa,ashwatthama,shakuni,duhshasana light=duryodhana}

While Virata was away chasing the Trigartas, [Duryodhana](duryodhana) came down on the Matsya country from the other side, with [Bhishma](bhishma), [Drona](drona), [Karna](karna), [Kripa](kripa), [Ashwatthama](ashwatthama), [Shakuni](shakuni), [Duhshasana](duhshasana) and many more. They drove off the cowherds and surrounded sixty thousand cattle with their chariots. {#p1 4.35}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=uttar light=uttar}

The chief cowherd raced to the city and found the only prince left there, [Uttara](uttar), also called Bhuminjaya. The king boasted of him in court, the cowherd said: now let the king's words be true. His bow was a vina, its arrows the notes; let him play it in the middle of the enemy. {#p2 4.35}

The prince answered among the women of the palace. He would go at once, if only he had a charioteer; his own had been killed in a battle that went on for a month. {#p3 4.36}

> The assembled Kurus shall witness my prowess today. And they shall say unto one another, 'Is it Arjuna himself who is opposing us?' {#q1 4.36}
> — Uttara

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,draupadi,uttar,uttara_f light=draupadi}

[Arjuna](arjuna) heard him, and quietly asked [Draupadi](draupadi) to tell the prince that Brihannala had once been Arjuna's charioteer. She could not bear to hear Uttara go on about Arjuna, and stepped out from among the women. {#p4 4.36}

> It was by him that the reins were held of Arjuna's excellent steeds when Agni consumed the forest of Khandava. {#q2 4.36}
> — Draupadi

Uttara could not bring himself to ask a eunuch. Let his sister ask, Draupadi said: Brihannala would do it for her. So the princess [Uttara](uttara_f), with her golden necklace and peacock plumes, ran to the dancing hall *like a flash of lightning rushing towards a mass of dark clouds*. If he would not drive for her brother, she said, she would give up her life. {#p5 4.37}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=arjuna,uttar,uttara_f light=arjuna}

> What ability have I to act as a charioteer in the field of battle? {#q3 4.37}
> — Arjuna

Singer or dancer, said the prince, just take the reins. So Arjuna played the fool for them: he tried to put on the coat of mail by raising it upside down, and the girls burst out laughing, until Uttara himself had to put the armour on him. The prince raised his lion banner and loaded the chariot with bows and arrows. {#p6 4.37}

The princess and her friends called after Brihannala: bring us fine cloths for our dolls, when you have beaten Bhishma and Drona. Arjuna laughed, in a voice *deep as the roar of the clouds*: if Uttara could beat those warriors, he would. The old women and the Brahmanas walked round the chariot to bless it, and wished Brihannala the victory Arjuna had won at Khandava. {#p7 4.37}
