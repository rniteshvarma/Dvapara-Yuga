When [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) saw Bhishma's array he went pale. How could they fight an army with the grandsire at its head, arrayed by every rule of the science? They had begun to doubt. [Arjuna](arjuna) told him how the few could beat the many. Once, before the war of gods and Asuras, Brahma had told Indra: {#p1 6.21}

> They that are desirous of victory do not conquer by might and energy so much as by truth, compassion, righteousness and energy. {#q1 6.21}
> — Brahma, as Arjuna tells it

And Narada had said, *there is victory where Krishna is*. Once, Arjuna said, Hari asked the gods and the Asuras who among them would win, and those who answered *with Krishna in front we will conquer* won the three worlds. The king had the lord of the gods himself wishing him victory; Arjuna saw no reason for grief. {#p2 6.21}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=yudhishthira,arjuna,krishna,bhima light=yudhishthira}

So Yudhishthira urged his men on: *fight fairly, desirous of the highest heaven*. Shikhandi stood in the centre under Arjuna's guard, Dhrishtadyumna in the van under Bhima's, Satyaki held the south. Yudhishthira rode a chariot fit for Indra, under a white umbrella with an ivory handle, while sages walked around him chanting, and he gave the Brahmanas cows, fruit, flowers, gold and cloth. {#p3 6.22}

Arjuna's car shone like a thousand suns, white horses yoked to it, [Krishna](krishna) at the reins. And at the front stood [Bhima](bhima), who could grind men, horses and elephants to dust with his bare hands; seeing him, the Kuru warriors *began to tremble like elephants sunk in mire*. {#p4 6.22}

Then Krishna pointed. {#p5 6.22}

> He, who scorching us with his wrath, stayeth in the midst of his forces, he, who will attack our troops like a lion, he, who performed three hundred horse-sacrifices. {#q2 6.22}
> — Krishna

That was Bhishma, Krishna said, ringed by warriors like the sun by clouds. Cut through them, and seek battle with him. {#p6 6.22}
