Neither the gods nor the great sages know his origin, [Krishna](krishna) said, for he is the origin of them all. Understanding and knowledge, patience and truth, pleasure and pain, birth and death, fear and fearlessness, fame and shame: all come from him. The wise, knowing this, think of him, talk of him to one another, and are content; and he, dwelling in their hearts, destroys their darkness with the shining lamp of knowledge. {#p1 6.34}

You are the supreme Brahman, [Arjuna](arjuna) said; Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa all say so, and now you say it yourself. Neither gods nor Danavas know you; only you know yourself. Tell me your glories, by which you fill these worlds; I never tire of hearing your words. {#p2 6.34}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna light=krishna}

There is no end to them, Krishna said, but he would name the chief. {#p3 6.34}

> I am the soul, O thou of curly hair, seated in the heart of every being, I am the beginning, and the middle, and the end also of all beings. {#q1 6.34}
> — Krishna

Among the Adityas he is Vishnu, among lights the sun, among the Vedas the Sama, among the senses the mind. He is Shankara among the Rudras, Kubera among the Yakshas, Meru among mountains, the ocean among waters, Bhrigu among sages, *Om* among words, the Himalaya among things that do not move. He is the fig tree among trees, Narada among celestial sages, Uchchaihshravas among horses, Airavata among elephants, the king among men, the thunderbolt among weapons. He is Vasuki among serpents, Yama among those who judge, Prahlada among the Daityas, Time among those who count, the lion among beasts, Garuda among birds, the wind among purifiers, Rama among warriors, the Ganga among rivers. Among letters he is *A*; among seasons, the flowering one. {#p4 6.34}

> I am the game of dice of them that cheat, and the splendour of those that are splendid. {#q2 6.34}
> — Krishna

He is Vasudeva among the Vrishnis and Dhananjaya among the sons of Pandu, Vyasa among sages, silence among secrets. {#p5 6.34}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,arjuna light=arjuna}

Whatever is glorious or mighty, he said, springs from a fragment of his splendour. But what need was there to know all this in detail? {#p6 6.34}

> Supporting this entire universe with only a portion (of myself), I stand. {#q3 6.34}
> — Krishna
