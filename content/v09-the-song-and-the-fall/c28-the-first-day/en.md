Duhshasana led out the Kuru troops with Bhishma at their head; the Pandavas came on with [Bhima](bhima) in front. Over the conchs and drums and the neighing of thousands of horses, Bhima roared like a bull, and the elephants and horses of the Kurus fouled themselves in fear. [Duryodhana](duryodhana) and his brothers ringed him with arrows like clouds round the sun, and the sons of Draupadi, Abhimanyu, the twins and Dhrishtadyumna fell on them. In that first clash no one on either side turned back. {#p1 6.44}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhishma,arjuna,abhimanyu,yudhishthira,shalya,drona,dhrishtadyumna light=bhishma}

Then they paired off, as Dhrishtadyumna had planned. [Bhishma](bhishma) and [Arjuna](arjuna), each wanting the other's life, could not make each other waver. Satyaki fought Kritavarma until both looked like blossoming *kinshuka* trees with wounds. [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu) fought Brihadbala of Kosala, who cut down his banner and charioteer; Abhimanyu cut down his. Bhima fought Duryodhana; Nakula Duhshasana; Sahadeva Durmukha. [Shalya](shalya) cut [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira)'s bow in two before his eyes. [Drona](drona) broke [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna)'s bow and sent an arrow into his body. {#p2 6.45}

Ghatotkacha fought the Rakshasa Alambusha; Shikhandi fought Ashwatthama; Virata fought Bhagadatta; Kripa and Brihatkshatra, their horses killed and bows cut, fought on with swords. Arjuna's son Iravan killed Shrutayush's horses; Uttara, Virata's son, fought Duryodhana's brother Viravahu. For a short while it was a beautiful sight. Then it became a melee, and no one could tell anything. {#p3 6.45}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhishma light=bhishma}

The fighting turned savage. Son did not know father, nor brother brother, nor nephew uncle. Elephants gored elephants and shrieked; horsemen leapt and struck off chariot-warriors' heads; men with their thighs broken called on their fathers and sons and asked for water, and some lay biting their lips and roaring with joy. {#p4 6.46}

> the sire slew the son, the son slew the sire, the sister's son slew the maternal uncle, the maternal uncle slew the sister's son, friend slew friend, and relatives slew kinsmen. {#q1 6.46}
> — Sanjaya

And as the Pandava divisions came near Bhishma they began to waver, and Bhishma under his silver palm-tree banner with its five stars shone *like the lunar orb under the peak of Meru*. {#p5 6.46}
