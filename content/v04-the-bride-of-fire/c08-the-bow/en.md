::scene{#s1 frame=draupadi,duryodhana,karna,shalya,ashwatthama light=draupadi}

The young princes stood up, in their ear-rings, each sure of his own skill and strength, brandishing their weapons, crying *Krishna shall be mine!* Proud of their looks, their birth, their wealth and their youth, they were like elephants in rut, and they looked at their best friends with jealousy now. {#p1 1.189}

The gods came to watch in their sky-chariots, with the Rudras, the Adityas, the Vasus and the Ashvins, Kubera and Yama, Gandharvas and apsaras, sages and serpents, so that the cars of heaven could hardly find a way through the crowded sky. {#p2 1.189}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,balarama,yudhishthira,bhima,arjuna,nakula,sahadeva light=krishna,balarama}

[Krishna](krishna) of the Vrishnis was there too, with [Balarama](balarama), watching. And he saw, among the Brahmanas, five young men looking at Draupadi like elephants at a lotus pool, or like fire covered in ash. {#p3 1.189}

> That is Yudhishthira; that is Bhima with Jishnu (Arjuna); and those are the twin heroes. {#q1 1.189}
> — Krishna

Balarama looked them over slowly and glanced back at his brother, satisfied. The kings, biting their lips, had eyes only for Draupadi, and did not notice the Pandavas at all. {#p4 1.189}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=1.189,1.191}
**Cousins.** Krishna and Balarama are the sons of Vasudeva, Kunti's brother. This is the first time in the epic that Krishna and the Pandavas are in the same place, and he knows them at a glance.
::

***

::scene{#s3 frame=duryodhana,shalya,ashwatthama,karna light=duryodhana,shalya}

One by one the princes came down to the bow: Karna, Duryodhana, Shalva, [Shalya](shalya), Ashwatthama and many more, kings of Kalinga and Banga, of the Pandyas and the Yavanas. Not one of them could string it. They strained with swollen lips, and the bow flung them on the ground, where they lay panting, their crowns and garlands knocked loose. They got up and went back sadly to their seats. {#p5 1.189}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=karna,draupadi,surya light=karna,draupadi}

Then [Karna](karna) went to the bow, raised it quickly, strung it and set the arrows to the string. The Pandavas, seeing the Sun's son take aim, thought the mark already as good as hit. {#p6 1.189}

> I will not select a Suta for my lord. {#q2 1.189}
> — Draupadi

Karna laughed in vexation, glanced up at the sun, and threw aside the bow he had already drawn to a circle. {#p7 1.189}

::aside{#a2 kind=versions src=1.189}
**A disputed passage.** Draupadi's refusal of Karna is in Ganguli's text, which follows the old printed editions of the epic. It is missing from many manuscripts, and the modern critical edition of the Mahabharata leaves it out of its main text as a doubtful passage. The scene has become one of the most famous in the whole story, so we keep it, but it is far from certain that it belongs to the oldest text.
::

***

::scene{#s5 frame=shishupala,jarasandha,shalya light=shishupala,jarasandha}

When all the Kshatriyas had given up, [Shishupala](shishupala), king of the Chedis, tried, and the bow brought him down on his knees. [Jarasandha](jarasandha), mighty as he was, stood before it a moment, motionless as a mountain; it threw him to his knees too, and he got up and left for his own kingdom. Shalya of Madra tried, and fell on his knees. All the kings in that great assembly had become a joke. {#p8 1.189}

***

::scene{#s6 frame=arjuna light=arjuna}

Then [Arjuna](arjuna) rose from among the Brahmanas. {#p9 1.190}

The Brahmanas shook their deer-skins and argued. Some were pleased and some were not. How could a Brahmana boy, unpractised and weak, string the bow that Shalya and the others could not? He would make every Brahmana in the place a laughing-stock. Stop him! But others said look at him: shoulders, arms and thighs like an elephant's trunk, the walk of a lion. He would not have stood up without the strength for it. And what is impossible for a Brahmana? Parashurama had defeated all the Kshatriyas; Agastya had drunk the ocean. *Let him try.* {#p10 1.190}

***

::scene{#s7 frame=arjuna,draupadi,drupada,shiva light=arjuna,draupadi}

Arjuna stood before the bow like a mountain. He walked round it, bowed his head to Shiva, the giver of boons, and thought of Krishna. Then he took it up. {#p11 1.190}

The bow that Rukmi, Sunitha, Vakra, Karna, Duryodhana and Shalya could not string, he strung in the blink of an eye. He took the five arrows and shot the mark, and it fell through the hole in the machine to the ground. {#p12 1.190}

The sky roared and the amphitheatre roared. The gods rained flowers on him; thousands of Brahmanas waved their upper cloths; the beaten kings cried out in grief; the musicians struck up and the bards began to sing. [Drupada](drupada) was overjoyed, and made up his mind to stand by this young man with his army if it came to that. {#p13 1.190}

In the uproar, Yudhishthira slipped away with the twins to their lodging. {#p14 1.190}

***

::scene{#s8 frame=draupadi,arjuna light=draupadi,arjuna}

[Draupadi](draupadi) saw the mark come down and the man who had brought it down, like Indra himself, and was glad. She went to him with a white robe and a garland. {#p15 1.190}

The Brahmanas saluted him, and Arjuna walked out of the lists with the woman who had just become his wife walking close behind him. {#p16 1.190}
