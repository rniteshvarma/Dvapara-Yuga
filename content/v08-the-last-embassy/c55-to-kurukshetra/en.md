::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,sahadeva,nakula light=yudhishthira}

[Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) told his brothers to draw up the army. There were seven armies, and seven leaders: Drupada, Virata, Dhrishtadyumna, Shikhandi, Satyaki, Chekitana and Bhima. But who should lead the seven, and stand against Bhishma, *like unto a fire having arrows for its flames*? {#p1 5.151}

[Sahadeva](sahadeva) named [Virata](virata), their kinsman, who had sheltered them in their distress. [Nakula](nakula) named [Drupada](drupada), their father-in-law, old, learned, surrounded by sons and grandsons like a tree of a hundred branches, who had done penance with his wife for Drona's death. {#p2 5.151}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,dhrishtadyumna,bhima,shikhandi light=dhrishtadyumna}

[Arjuna](arjuna) named [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna), born from the sacrificial fire armed and armoured on his chariot, born to destroy Drona: no one else could bear Bhishma's arrows, which only Parashurama had borne before. [Bhima](bhima) named [Shikhandi](shikhandi), born, the sages said, to kill Bhishma. {#p3 5.151}

Yudhishthira left the choice to Krishna. Their lives, their kingdom, their joy and sorrow were in him. {#p4 5.151}

> By that we have been freed from the debt we owed to virtue. Fault-finding persons will not be able to reproach us for anything. {#q1 5.151}
> — Krishna

He approved of them all, Krishna said; but his choice was Dhrishtadyumna. {#p5 5.151}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhima,yudhishthira,draupadi light=bhima}

The kings shouted for joy, and the camp rang with *Draw up! Draw up!* The army moved like the Ganga in flood. Bhima and the twins, Abhimanyu, the sons of Draupadi and Dhrishtadyumna went in the van; Yudhishthira marched in the middle with the stores, the carts, the treasure, the surgeons and the sick. [Draupadi](draupadi) stayed behind at Upaplavya with the women of the household, under guard. {#p6 5.152}

> And hearing the blare of the conch called Panchajanya, which resembled the roll of the thunder, all the warriors (of the Pandava army) were filled with joy. {#q2 5.152}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=yudhishthira,krishna,satyaki light=krishna}

Yudhishthira chose a level, cool part of the plain, with grass and firewood, keeping clear of cemeteries, temples, hermitages and sacred ground. Krishna and Arjuna rode about scattering the Kuru outposts; Dhrishtadyumna and [Satyaki](satyaki) measured out the camp. By the clear river Hiranvati, Krishna had a moat dug and set guards on it. {#p7 5.153}

Tents went up for the allied kings by the thousand, each stocked like a palace. In every pavilion there were bows and bowstrings and armour heaped like hills, honey and ghee, lac, water and fodder, craftsmen on wages and physicians with all their medicines, and elephants armoured in spiked steel. {#p8 5.153}
