::scene{#s1 frame=bhishma,drona,duryodhana light=bhishma}

On the third morning [Bhishma](bhishma) drew the Kuru army up in the shape of Garuda. He himself was the beak; [Drona](drona) and Kritavarma were its eyes; Ashwatthama and Kripa its head; Bhurishravas, Shalya, Bhagadatta and Jayadratha its neck; [Duryodhana](duryodhana) with his followers its back; the Avantis, the Kambojas and the Shakas its tail; the Magadhas and Kalingas its right wing, Brihadbala and his peoples its left. {#p1 6.56}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,bhima,yudhishthira,krishna light=arjuna}

Against it [Arjuna](arjuna) and Dhrishtadyumna formed the half-moon. [Bhima](bhima) held its right horn with many kings; then Virata and Drupada, Nila with his poisoned weapons, and Dhrishtaketu with the Chedis and Kashis. Dhrishtadyumna and Shikhandi held the middle with [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) among his elephants, then Satyaki, the sons of Draupadi, Iravan, Ghatotkacha and the Kekayas; and on the left horn stood Arjuna, with [Krishna](krishna), *protector of the whole Universe*, as his guard. {#p2 6.56}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,bhima light=bhima}

Arjuna felled the leaders of chariot divisions like the Destroyer at the end of an age, and still the Kurus fought on, breaking the Pandava ranks and being broken. Dust hid the sun; men fought by colours, watchwords, names and tribes. Neither array broke: Drona held the one, Arjuna and Bhima the other. Horsemen cut down horsemen, elephant-riders chariot-warriors, foot-soldiers horsemen; the earth grew so thick with blood that the dust disappeared and the sky cleared, and headless trunks stood up all over the field. {#p3 6.57}

Then Duryodhana with a thousand chariots charged the Pandavas and Ghatotkacha; the Pandavas went for Bhishma and Drona; Arjuna went for the kings; and Abhimanyu and Satyaki went for Shakuni's army. {#p4 6.57}
