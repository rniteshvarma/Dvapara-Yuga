::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,arjuna,draupadi light=yudhishthira}

They girded on their swords and their lizard-skin finger-guards and went south to the Yamuna, through Surasena, with Panchala on their right and Dasharna on their left. Bearded and thin, they told people they were hunters. {#p1 4.5}

When they reached the Matsya country, [Draupadi](draupadi) could go no further. *Great is my fatigue.* So [Arjuna](arjuna) picked her up *like the leader of a herd of elephants*, carried her to the edge of the city, and set her down. {#p2 4.5}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=yudhishthira,arjuna light=arjuna}

Where should they leave their weapons? asked [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira). If they walked in armed they would frighten the citizens, and everyone knew the Gandiva. If even one of them was recognised, it would be twelve more years in the forest. {#p3 4.5}

> Hard by yon cemetery and near that inaccessible peak is a mighty Sami tree, throwing-about its gigantic branches and difficult to ascend. {#q1 4.5}
> — Arjuna

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,yudhishthira,bhima,nakula,sahadeva light=nakula}

So each of them unstrung his bow: the Gandiva, with which Arjuna had beaten gods and men and serpents; Yudhishthira's bow; Bhima's; Nakula's, which had conquered the west; Sahadeva's, which had conquered the south. They bundled them with their swords, quivers and razor-sharp arrows, and [Nakula](nakula) climbed the tree and tied them where the branches would not break and the rain would not reach. {#p4 4.5}

Then they hung a corpse in the tree, so that people would smell it and keep away. When herdsmen asked about it, they said: {#p5 4.5}

> This is our mother, aged one hundred and eighty years. We have hung up her dead body, in accordance with the custom observed by our forefathers. {#q2 4.5}
> — the Pandavas

***

::scene{#s4 frame=yudhishthira light=yudhishthira}

Among themselves, for the year of hiding, Yudhishthira gave them secret names: Jaya, Jayanta, Vijaya, Jayatsena and Jayadvala. Then they walked into the great city of Virata. {#p6 4.5}
