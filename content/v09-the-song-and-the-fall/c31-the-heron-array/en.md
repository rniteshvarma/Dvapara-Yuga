That night [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) went to [Krishna](krishna) with his brothers and the kings, sick with grief. Bhishma was consuming his army like fire in dry grass; Yama, Indra, Varuna or Kubera might be beaten, but not Bhishma. {#p1 6.50}

> Such being the case, I am sinking in the fathomless ocean represented by Bhishma, without a boat (to rescue me). {#q1 6.50}
> — Yudhishthira

Better to go to the forest and do penance for the rest of his life than to send these kings to their deaths. Bhima alone fought with all his strength, and even Bhima could not destroy that army in a hundred years. {#p2 6.50}

> As regards Arjuna, I see that he is an indifferent spectator in this battle. {#q2 6.50}
> — Yudhishthira

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,yudhishthira,dhrishtadyumna,shikhandi light=dhrishtadyumna}

Do not grieve, Krishna said. Your brothers are heroes; Satyaki, Virata, Drupada and Dhrishtadyumna are with you, and Shikhandi, who is certainly the slayer of Bhishma. So Yudhishthira turned to [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna) before them all: you are our commander, as Kartikeya was the gods'; lead, and we will follow. {#p3 6.50}

> Ordained of old by Sambhu himself, I am, O son of Pritha, the slayer of Drona. {#q3 6.50}
> — Dhrishtadyumna

He would fight Bhishma, Drona, Kripa, Shalya, Jayadratha and all the proud kings, he said, and the Pandava warriors shouted. {#p4 6.50}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,drupada,yudhishthira light=arjuna}

Yudhishthira asked for the *Krauncha*, the heron array, which Brihaspati once taught Indra in the war of gods and Asuras. At dawn Dhrishtadyumna drew it up. [Arjuna](arjuna) went at its head, his ape banner made by the divine artificer seeming to dance through the sky; Drupada was its head, Kuntibhoja and Shaibya its eyes, the Dasharnas and Kiratas its neck, Yudhishthira and the many peoples with him its wings, Virata with the Kekayas and the kings of Kashi and Chedi its rear. Elephants marched in the joints of the wings like blazing mountains. {#p5 6.50}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=duryodhana,bhishma,drona light=duryodhana}

Seeing it, [Duryodhana](duryodhana) told his warriors that each of them alone could kill the Pandavas, and set his peoples around Bhishma to guard him. [Bhishma](bhishma) led with a great host, [Drona](drona) behind him, Shakuni guarding Drona; Duryodhana and his brothers in the middle; Bhurishravas, Shalya and Bhagadatta on the left, Somadatta, Susharma and Sudakshina on the right, Ashwatthama, Kripa and Kritavarma in the rear. Bhishma roared and blew his conch, and once more Panchajanya, Devadatta, Paundra and the rest answered, and the two armies went at each other again. {#p6 6.51}
