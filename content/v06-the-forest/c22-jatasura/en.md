::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,nakula,sahadeva,draupadi light=yudhishthira}

Among the Brahmanas who lived with the Pandavas at Badari was one who said he was skilled in counsel and learned in all the scriptures. [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) fed him and trusted him. His name was Jatasura, and he was a Rakshasa, *like unto a fire covered with ashes*. He wanted the Pandavas' bows and quivers, and he was waiting for a chance to carry off Draupadi. {#p1 3.156}

One day, when Bhima was out hunting, Ghatotkacha's Rakshasas had scattered, and Lomasa and the sages were away bathing and gathering flowers, Jatasura took his true shape. He seized the weapons and [Draupadi](draupadi), and ran off with Yudhishthira and the twins. {#p2 3.156}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=sahadeva,yudhishthira,draupadi light=sahadeva}

[Sahadeva](sahadeva) struggled free, snatched the sword called Kaushika from the demon, and shouted for Bhima. Yudhishthira lectured the Rakshasa on dharma as he was carried, and then made himself so heavy that Jatasura could hardly move. *Do ye not entertain any fear*, he told the others. *I have checked his speed.* {#p3 3.156}

But Sahadeva wanted to fight. {#p4 3.156}

> If the sun sets to-day, the Rakshasa living yet, O Bharata, I will not any more say that I am a Kshatriya. {#q1 3.156}
> — Sahadeva

*Ho! Ho! Rakshasa, say! I am Pandu's son, Sahadeva. Either, after having killed me, carry off this lady, or being slain, lie senseless here.* {#p5 3.156}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhima,sahadeva light=bhima}

Just then [Bhima](bhima) came up with his mace in his hand. He had suspected the false Brahmana for some time, he said, from the way he looked over their weapons; but he had been a guest, and done them no harm, so Bhima had let him live. Now it was different. {#p6 3.156}

They fought with trees until the forest around them was bare, and then hand to hand. At last Bhima threw him down, broke his limbs, and struck off his head with his elbow, *like unto a fruit from its stem*. The Brahmanas praised Bhima as the Maruts praise Indra. {#p7 3.156}
