::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,bhima,arjuna,nakula,sahadeva,virata light=yudhishthira}

On the third day the five brothers bathed, put on white robes and every ornament, and with [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) at their head walked into Virata's council hall *like five intoxicated elephants*. They sat down on the thrones kept for kings, and shone like fires on an altar. When [Virata](virata) came in to hold his council, he stopped, and grew angry. {#p1 4.70}

> A player at dice thou wert employed by me as a courtier! How couldst thou occupy the royal seat thus attired in handsome robes and ornaments? {#q1 4.70}
> — Virata

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,yudhishthira light=arjuna}

[Arjuna](arjuna) smiled, wanting to have some fun with him. {#p2 4.70}

> This person, O king, deserveth to occupy the same seat with Indra himself. {#q2 4.70}
> — Arjuna

He went on: devoted to Brahmanas, learned in the Vedas, master of weapons beyond any god or man; once followed by ten thousand elephants and thirty thousand golden chariots, praised by eight hundred bards, paid tribute by every king, feeding eighty-eight thousand Snatakas, guarding the old, the blind and the helpless as his own sons. *Kind unto all creatures he is no other than the bull of the Kuru race, king Yudhishthira the just.* Did he not deserve a royal seat? {#p3 4.70}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,bhima,nakula,sahadeva,draupadi,virata light=draupadi}

Then which was Arjuna, asked Virata, and which Bhima, Nakula, Sahadeva? Where was Draupadi? {#p4 4.71}

The cook Vallava was [Bhima](bhima), Arjuna told him, who had killed the Rakshasas on Gandhamadana for Krishnaa's flowers, and was the Gandharva who killed Kichaka. The horse-keeper was [Nakula](nakula), the cowherd [Sahadeva](sahadeva). And the queen's Sairindhri was Drupada's daughter, [Draupadi](draupadi), for whose sake the Kichakas died. {#p5 4.71}

> I am, O king, Arjuna who, it is evident, thou hast heard, is that son of Pritha, who is Bhima's junior and the senior of the twins! We have, O king, happily passed in thy abode the period of non-discovery, like infants in the womb! {#q3 4.71}
> — Arjuna

[Uttara](uttar) pointed each one out again, and then told his father what Arjuna had done: the elephant killed with one arrow, the cattle recovered, Bhishma, Drona and Duryodhana beaten. *That achievement is his and not mine.* {#p6 4.71}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=virata,yudhishthira,arjuna,uttara_f light=virata}

Virata, knowing now whom he had struck, said it was time to make amends. Bhima had rescued him, Arjuna had brought back his cattle. He embraced them all again and again, smelt their heads, and could not look at them enough. He offered Yudhishthira his whole kingdom, sceptre, treasury and capital. And let Arjuna take his daughter [Uttara](uttara_f) as his wife. {#p7 4.71}

Yudhishthira looked at Arjuna. {#p8 4.71}

> O monarch, I accept thy daughter as my daughter-in-law. And alliance of this kind between the Matsya and the Bharatas is, indeed, desirable. {#q4 4.71}
> — Arjuna
