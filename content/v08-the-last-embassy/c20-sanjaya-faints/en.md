What was Yudhishthira doing? asked [Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra). All the Panchalas looked to his face, said [Sanjaya](sanjaya), and he held them back. Chariots came in from every side to cheer him; the Kekayas, the Matsyas, even the cowherds rejoiced; Brahmana and Kshatriya girls came to see Partha in his armour. {#p1 5.50}

Then tell me their forces, said the king. {#p2 5.50}

> Thus interrogated, in the midst of the Kurus and in their very hall, the son of Gavalgana became thoughtful for a moment and seemed to draw repeatedly deep and long sighs; and suddenly he fell down in a swoon without any apparent reason. {#q1 5.50}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=vidura,dhritarashtra light=vidura}

[Vidura](vidura) called out that Sanjaya had fallen senseless. *Without doubt*, said Dhritarashtra, *Sanjaya, having seen those mighty car-warriors, the sons of Kunti, hath his mind filled with great anxiety.* {#p3 5.50}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=sanjaya,dhrishtadyumna,yudhishthira,bhima,arjuna,nakula,sahadeva,shikhandi,abhimanyu,krishna light=sanjaya}

When he came to, Sanjaya named them. He had seen the sons of Kunti thinned by their year of hiding. With [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna) as their ally they would fight; with [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira), who never forsook truth; with [Bhima](bhima), who carried his brothers out of the house of lac, saved them from Hidimba and from Jayadratha, and killed the Krodhavashas on Gandhamadana; with [Arjuna](arjuna), who fought Indra at Khandava and Mahadeva himself; with [Nakula](nakula), who conquered the west, and [Sahadeva](sahadeva), equal to only four men on earth. {#p4 5.50}

> She, who, while living of yore as the daughter of the king of Kasi, had practised the austerest penances; she, who, O bull of the Bharata race, desiring even in a subsequent life to compass the destruction of Bhishma, took her birth as the daughter of Panchala, and accidentally became afterwards a male. {#q2 5.50}
> — Sanjaya, of Shikhandi

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=5.50}
This is the first open mention, in the Kuru court, of [Shikhandi](shikhandi)'s past as Amba, the princess of Kashi. The full story is told later in the Udyoga Parva.
::

And the Kekaya brothers, Satyaki, Virata, the king of Kashi, Draupadi's sons, [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu), Dhrishtaketu of the Chedis, Jarasandha's son, Drupada, and [Krishna](krishna), *the refuge of the Pandavas, even as Vasava is of the celestials*. {#p5 5.50}
