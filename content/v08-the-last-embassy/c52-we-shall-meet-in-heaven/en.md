[Krishna](krishna) smiled. Did the means of winning an empire not tempt him? Did he not want the whole earth, given by Krishna's own hand? Then the Pandavas' victory was certain. {#p1 5.142}

When [Karna](karna) saw Arjuna in battle on his white-horsed chariot with Krishna driving, and heard Gandiva's twang like thunder; when he saw Yudhishthira burning up his enemies, and Bhima dancing after drinking Duhshasana's blood, and the twins breaking the Kuru ranks, then every sign of the Krita, the Treta and the Dvapara ages would be gone. {#p2 5.142}

> Seven days after, will be the day of the new moon. Let the battle commence then, for that day, it hath been said, is presided over by Indra. {#q1 5.142}
> — Krishna

Let Karna tell Drona, Bhishma and Kripa that this was a pleasant month: food, drink and fuel plentiful, no flies, the roads dry, the weather neither hot nor cold. And let him tell the kings that their wish would be fully granted; all who followed Duryodhana would die by weapons and reach a high state. {#p3 5.142}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=karna,krishna light=karna}

Karna bowed to him. Why did Krishna, who knew everything, try to beguile him? The earth was going to its end because of Shakuni, Duhshasana, Duryodhana and himself. {#p4 5.143}

He had seen the signs: Saturn pressing on Rohini, Mars turning toward Anuradha, a spot shifting on the moon. Duryodhana's horses wept and would not eat; his drums would not sound, while the Pandavas' drums sounded unstruck; the gods rained flesh and blood on the Kuru camp; birds with one wing, one eye and one leg screamed over it. {#p5 5.143}

And he had had a dream. {#p6 5.143}

> I have in a vision, O Achyuta, beheld Yudhishthira ascending with his brothers a palace supported by a thousand columns. All of them appeared with white head-gears and in white robes. {#q2 5.143}
> — Karna

Krishna was wrapping the bloodied earth in weapons; Yudhishthira sat on a heap of bones eating rice and milk from a golden cup, and swallowed the earth that Krishna handed him; Bhima stood on a summit with his mace as if devouring the earth; Arjuna sat with Krishna on a white elephant. On the Kuru side only [Ashwatthama](ashwatthama), Kripa and Kritavarma wore white turbans; every other king wore red. And Bhishma, Drona, Duryodhana and Karna himself rode in a cart drawn by camels toward the south, Agastya's quarter, the direction of Yama. {#p7 5.143}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=karna,krishna light=karna}

> Indeed, the destruction of the earth is at hand when my words, O Karna, do not become acceptable to thy heart. {#q3 5.143}
> — Krishna

> If, O Krishna, we come out of this great battle that will be so destructive of heroic Kshatriyas, with life, then, O thou of mighty arms may we meet here again. Otherwise, O Krishna, we shall certainly meet in heaven. {#q4 5.143}
> — Karna

Then Karna held Krishna tightly to his chest, stepped down from the chariot, and rode back with Sanjaya and the others on his own golden car, very sad. {#p8 5.143}
