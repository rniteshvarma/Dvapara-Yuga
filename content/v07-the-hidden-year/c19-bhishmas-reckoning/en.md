[Bhishma](bhishma) made peace. Drona's son was right and so was Kripa, he said; Karna had only been trying to raise their spirits. But this was no time for quarrels. Bhishma had never heard of the Vedas and the Brahma weapon dwelling together in anyone but the teacher of the Bharatas and his son. {#p1 4.51}

> Of all the calamities that may befall an army that have been enumerated by men of wisdom, the worst is disunion among the leaders. {#q1 4.51}
> — Bhishma

[Ashwatthama](ashwatthama) said only that even an enemy's virtues should be admitted, and it was his father, angry, who had praised Arjuna. [Duryodhana](duryodhana), with Karna, Kripa and Bhishma, made peace with [Drona](drona). The teacher said he had already been appeased by Bhishma's first words. But they must keep Arjuna from reaching Duryodhana, and Bhishma must now say truly whether the year was over. {#p2 4.51}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhishma light=bhishma}

> The wheel of time revolves with its divisions, viz., with Kalas and Kasthas and Muhurtas and days and fortnights and months and constellations and planets and seasons and years. {#q2 4.52}
> — Bhishma

Because the heavenly bodies do not keep perfect step, he said, two months are added every five years. {#p3 4.52}

> It seems to me that calculating this wise, there would be an excess of five months and twelve nights in thirteen years. Everything, therefore, that the sons of Pandu had promised, hath been exactly fulfilled by them. {#q3 4.52}
> — Bhishma

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=4.52}
Bhishma seems to be reckoning by lunar months, which fall behind the solar year and are made up with extra months. By his count, the Pandavas' thirteen years were complete before Arjuna showed himself.
::

The Pandavas would rather die than lie, he said. Had they wanted their kingdom by unfair means, they would have fought at the dice game. And when the time came they would not give up what was theirs even if Indra defended it. No one ever went into a battle sure of winning. {#p4 4.52}

> I will not, O grandsire, give back the Pandavas their kingdom. Let every preparation, therefore, for battle be made without delay. {#q4 4.52}
> — Duryodhana

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhishma,duryodhana,drona,ashwatthama,kripa,karna light=bhishma}

Then Bhishma gave his orders. Duryodhana should go back towards the capital with a quarter of the army, and another quarter should escort the cattle. With the other half, he, Drona, Karna, Ashwatthama and Kripa would hold off Arjuna, or Virata, or Indra himself. He set them in order: Drona in the centre, Ashwatthama on the left, Kripa on the right, Karna in front in his armour, and Bhishma himself guarding the rear. {#p5 4.52}
