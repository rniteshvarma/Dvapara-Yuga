How did Drona and Dhrishtadyumna fight? [Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra) asked. He had come to think destiny stronger than effort, since even Bhishma, who could destroy every creature when angry, could not get past the son of Pandu. {#p1 6.53}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=drona,dhrishtadyumna light=drona}

[Drona](drona) felled [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna)'s charioteer and wounded his horses; Dhrishtadyumna struck back with nine arrows and called *Wait, wait*. Then Drona fitted an arrow like the thunderbolt, like a second rod of Death, and both armies cried out. Dhrishtadyumna stood alone and unmoved as a mountain and cut it down in the air. {#p2 6.53}

He hurled a gold dart set with lapis; Drona, smiling, cut it in three. He hurled a mace heavy as a mountain; Drona swerved his chariot and let it pass, and his arrows went through Dhrishtadyumna's mail and drank his blood. Both men, covered in blood, looked like two flowering *kinshuka* trees. {#p3 6.53}

Drona cut his bow again, killed his horses and charioteer, cut the guard from his hand. Dhrishtadyumna jumped down with a mace, and Drona cut the mace to pieces before his feet touched the ground. Then he took a great shield bright with a hundred moons and a beautiful sword, and rushed at Drona *like a hungry lion in the forest towards an infuriate elephant*. Drona held him there with arrows alone, and Dhrishtadyumna stood warding them off with his shield and could come no nearer. {#p4 6.53}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhima,dhrishtadyumna,drona light=bhima}

Then [Bhima](bhima) came up, struck Drona with seven arrows, and had Dhrishtadyumna taken up onto another chariot. Duryodhana sent the king of the Kalingas with a great division against Bhima; Drona turned on Virata and Drupada, and Dhrishtadyumna went to support Yudhishthira. And between the Kalingas and Bhima began a battle *destructive of the universe*. {#p5 6.53}
