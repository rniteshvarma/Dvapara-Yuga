::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,bhima,arjuna,nakula,sahadeva,draupadi light=yudhishthira}

The Pandavas went along the shore of the western sea, from one sacred place to the next, giving away cattle, until they came to Prabhasa. There [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) bathed with his brothers and Draupadi, and for twelve days lived on air and water, with fires burning around him on every side. {#p1 3.118}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=balarama,krishna,samba,satyaki,yudhishthira,draupadi light=balarama}

When the Vrishnis heard, [Balarama](balarama) and [Krishna](krishna) came with their army, and [Samba](samba), and [Satyaki](satyaki). They saw the Pandavas lying on the bare ground, covered in dust, and Draupadi worn and sad, and they wept aloud. {#p2 3.118}

Then Balarama, white as milk and the moon, with his wreath of wild flowers and his plough, spoke to Krishna. {#p3 3.119}

> O Krishna, I do not see that the practice of virtue leads to any good or that unrighteous practices can cause evil. {#q1 3.119}
> — Balarama

Here was Yudhishthira in bark and matted hair, and Duryodhana ruling the earth, and the ground had not swallowed him. How could Bhishma and Drona and Kripa live happily? How would the blind king face his ancestors? Look at Bhima, who conquered the east, starving; Sahadeva, who conquered the south, dressed as a hermit; Draupadi, born from the altar fire. *Why did not the earth subside with all its hills?* {#p4 3.119}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=satyaki,pradyumna,samba,abhimanyu,aniruddha light=satyaki}

[Satyaki](satyaki) said it was no time for weeping. Yudhishthira had friends who could protect the three worlds; why should they wait for him to speak? Let the Yadava army march today. [Pradyumna](pradyumna) would cut down Kripa, Drona and Karna; Samba would deal with Duhshasana; [Aniruddha](aniruddha) would cover the earth with the heads of Dhritarashtra's sons. And [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu) could rule the kingdom until Yudhishthira's vow was done. {#p5 3.120}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=krishna,yudhishthira light=krishna,yudhishthira}

Krishna said Satyaki was right, but Yudhishthira would never accept a kingdom he had not won with his own arms; nothing would make him break the rules of his order. {#p6 3.120}

> But to me truth seems to be the first consideration, above that of my sovereign power itself. {#q2 3.120}
> — Yudhishthira

*But it is Krishna alone who precisely knoweth what I am; and it is I alone who precisely know what Krishna (really) is.* When the time came, Krishna would know it. Let the Dasharhas go home. They embraced, and the Yadavas went back to Dvaraka, and the Pandavas went on to the river Payoshni. {#p7 3.120}
