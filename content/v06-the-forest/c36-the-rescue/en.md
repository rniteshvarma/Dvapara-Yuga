Out in the forest, [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) heard the birds and beasts crying, all fleeing toward the sun. Someone had come into the forest, he said. *My heart aches and seems to burn!* They gave up the hunt and drove for home, and on the way a jackal howled on their left. {#p1 3.267}

At the hermitage they found Draupadi's maid Dhatreyika lying on the ground weeping. Jayadratha had taken her, she said, and the broken branches along his track were still fresh. Go quickly, before some worthless man touches her lips *like a dog licking clarified butter kept in the sacrificial pot*. {#p2 3.267}

*Retire, good woman, and control thy tongue*, said Yudhishthira. And they followed the track, hissing like snakes and twanging their bows, until they saw the dust of Jayadratha's army, and Dhaumya among the soldiers, urging Bhima to hurry. {#p3 3.267}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=jayadratha,draupadi light=draupadi}

When [Jayadratha](jayadratha) saw their banners he lost heart, and asked [Draupadi](draupadi), seated in his chariot, which husband was which. {#p4 3.268}

> Having done this violent deed calculated to shorten thy life, what will it avail thee now, O fool, to know the names of those great warriors? {#q1 3.268}
> — Draupadi

But since he was about to die, she said, she would tell him, as the custom was. And she named them one by one: Yudhishthira under his banner with its two drums; Bhima, *He never forgetteth a foe*; Arjuna, the first of bowmen; Nakula with his sword; Sahadeva, wise and dear to Kunti, who would sooner die than speak against dharma. {#p5 3.268}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhima,arjuna,nakula,sahadeva,yudhishthira light=bhima}

Then the brothers fell on his army. [Bhima](bhima) killed an elephant and its riders and fourteen foot-soldiers with his mace; [Arjuna](arjuna) killed five hundred mountaineers; Yudhishthira killed a hundred Sauviras in the twinkling of an eye, and shot the king of Trigarta through the chest. [Nakula](nakula) leapt down with his sword and scattered heads like a farmer sowing seed. Bhima killed Kotika, who had first spoken to Draupadi. Arjuna cut down the twelve Sauvira standard-bearers. {#p6 3.268}

Jayadratha saw his army dying, set Draupadi down from his chariot, and ran for his life into the forest. {#p7 3.269}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=yudhishthira,draupadi,bhima,arjuna light=yudhishthira}

Yudhishthira took Draupadi back to the hermitage with Dhaumya and the twins. But Bhima and Arjuna went after Jayadratha. *Remembering (our sister) Dussala and the celebrated Gandhari*, Yudhishthira told them, do not kill him. {#p8 3.269}

> If you care to do what is agreeable to me, you must slay that mean and despicable wretch. {#q2 3.269}
> — Draupadi
