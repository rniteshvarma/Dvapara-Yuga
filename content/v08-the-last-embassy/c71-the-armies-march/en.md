::scene{#s1 frame=duryodhana,drona,bhishma light=duryodhana}

Next morning, under a cloudless sky, the kings of [Duryodhana](duryodhana)'s army bathed, put on garlands and white robes, poured libations on the fire, took the Brahmanas' blessings, and raised their banners. [Drona](drona) led the first division, with Vinda and Anuvinda of Avanti, the Kekayas and the Bahlikas. [Bhishma](bhishma), Ashwatthama, Jayadratha, Shakuni and the kings of the south, west, north and east, Shakas, Kiratas and Yavanas, came in the second. Kritavarma, the Trigartas, Shalya, Bhurishravas and Duryodhana with his brothers came last. {#p1 5.198}

At the far end of Kurukshetra Duryodhana raised a camp so splendid that {#p2 5.198}

> even those that were clever among the citizens of Hastinapura could not distinguish their city from the encampment. {#q1 5.198}

Tents by the thousand covered five yojanas of the field, stocked for the kings and their armies, and he made provision for all the rest: craftsmen, bards and singers, traders, prostitutes, spies, and those who had only come to watch. {#p3 5.198}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=yudhishthira,dhrishtadyumna,bhima,abhimanyu light=yudhishthira}

[Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) too ordered his warriors out under [Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna), and they shone in their golden earrings *like fires on the sacrificial altar when fed with clarified butter*. To confuse the Kurus, he arranged his divisions in a new order. [Bhima](bhima) led the first, with the sons of Draupadi, [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu), the twins and the Prabhadrakas. In the middle went Virata and the Panchala princes, and with them [Krishna](krishna) and [Arjuna](arjuna). Yudhishthira came behind with the kings, Chekitana, Dhrishtaketu and Satyaki, and then, slowly, with the carts and treasure, the sick and the weak, guarded by elephants. {#p4 5.199}

> And the warriors by thousands and ten thousands, filled with joy, beat their drums by thousands and blew conchs by tens of thousands! {#q2 5.199}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=5.199}
Here the Udyoga Parva ends. The next book, the Bhishma Parva, opens with both armies facing each other on Kurukshetra and with the Bhagavad Gita; it is Volume IX, *The Song and the Fall*.
