::scene{#s1 frame=dhritarashtra,sanjaya light=dhritarashtra}

The news of Sweta's death pleased [Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra), and he felt no shame. Bhishma and Drona had not wanted the war, nor Kripa, nor Gandhari, nor he himself, he said; Duryodhana would not listen. But he was afraid of Arjuna. What had Arjuna done after Sweta fell? What had Dhrishtadyumna done? {#p1 6.49}

> As is the construction of an embankment when the waters have disappeared, so is thy understanding, or, it is like the digging of a well when house is on fire. {#q1 6.49}
> — Sanjaya

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhishma,arjuna,shalya light=bhishma}

That afternoon, Sanjaya said, Sweta's brother Shankha, another son of Virata, blazed up in turn and drove at Shalya, and seven Kuru warriors closed round Shalya again. [Bhishma](bhishma), roaring like the clouds, took up his six-cubit bow and came at Shankha, and the Pandava army shook like a boat in a storm. [Arjuna](arjuna) put himself in front of Shankha, and the grandsire and Arjuna fought. Shalya leapt down with his mace and killed Shankha's horses; Shankha ran with his sword to Arjuna's chariot and was safe. {#p2 6.49}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhishma,drupada light=bhishma}

Then Bhishma left Arjuna and fell on Drupada and his Panchalas, and on the Matsyas, the Kekayas and the Prabhadrakas. He stood *like a blazing fire without smoke*, like the midday sun; no Pandava warrior could look at him. {#p3 6.49}

> that hero of rigid vows slew Pandava car-warriors, naming each, O Bharata, beforehand. {#q2 6.49}
> — Sanjaya

The Pandava host looked round for a protector and found none, like cattle in the cold. Then the sun set, and seeing Bhishma still standing proud on the field, the Pandavas withdrew their troops for the night. So ended the first day. {#p4 6.49}

::aside{#a1 kind=versions src=6.49}
The text is not consistent here. The previous section ends with Arjuna and Krishna withdrawing the troops for the night after Sweta's death; this one takes up the same afternoon again with Shankha, and only then does the sun set.
::
