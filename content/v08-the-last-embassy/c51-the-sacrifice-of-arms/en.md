What had Krishna said to Karna in the chariot? [Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra) wanted to know, and [Sanjaya](sanjaya) told him. {#p1 5.140}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,karna,kunti light=krishna}

[Krishna](krishna) began with the law. [Karna](karna) had sat with learned Brahmanas and knew the scriptures: a son born to an unmarried girl belongs to the man who marries her. {#p2 5.140}

> Thou, O Karna, hast been born in this way. Thou art, therefore, morally the son of Pandu. Come, be a king, according to the injunction of the scriptures. {#q1 5.140}
> — Krishna

Let the Pandavas know him as Kunti's son, born before Yudhishthira. The five brothers and the sons of Draupadi and Subhadra would clasp his feet, and so would every king on their side. Draupadi too would come to him in the sixth turn. Krishna himself would anoint him lord of the earth; Yudhishthira would be his heir and ride behind him holding the white fan, Bhima would hold the umbrella over his head, and Arjuna would drive his chariot. {#p3 5.140}

> Let the Pandavas proclaim,—Victory to Vasusena. Surrounded by the Pandavas, like the moon by the stars, rule thou the kingdom, O son of Kunti, and gladden Kunti herself. {#q2 5.140}
> — Krishna

***

::scene{#s3 frame=karna,surya,kunti,adhiratha,radha light=karna}

Karna said he knew all of it. Kunti had conceived him by [Surya](surya) as a girl and cast him away the day he was born. It was [Adhiratha](adhiratha) the charioteer who took him home, and [Radha](radha)'s breasts had filled with milk for him that very day, and she had cleaned him as a baby. How could he deny her the offerings due to a mother? Adhiratha had performed his childhood rites and named him Vasusena; he had married the wives Adhiratha chose and had sons and grandsons by them. {#p4 5.141}

> From joy or fear. O Govinda. I cannot venture to destroy those bonds even for the sake of the whole earth or heaps of gold. {#q3 5.141}
> — Karna

Duryodhana had gone to war trusting in him, and had chosen him to meet Arjuna in single combat. He would not play him false, not for death or kinship or fear or any temptation. {#p5 5.141}

And let this talk be kept secret. If Yudhishthira learned that Karna was Kunti's eldest son, he would never take the kingdom; and if the kingdom came to Karna, he would give it straight to Duryodhana. Let Yudhishthira be king for ever. {#p6 5.141}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=karna,krishna,arjuna,bhima light=karna}

Then Karna described the war that was coming as a great sacrifice that Duryodhana would perform. Krishna would oversee it; Arjuna would be the Hotri, Gandiva the ladle, the warriors' valour the butter poured on the fire. Bhima would chant, Yudhishthira would preside, the twins would slay the victims, and the army would be Duryodhana's wife. {#p7 5.141}

> For those harsh words, O Krishna, that I said before unto the sons of Pandu for the gratification of Dhritarashtra's son,—for that wicked conduct of mine,—I am consumed with repentance. {#q4 5.141}
> — Karna

When Arjuna killed him, the sacrifice would be piled again; when Bhima drank Duhshasana's blood, the Soma would be drunk; when Bhima killed Duryodhana, it would end. And when the widows wept with Gandhari among the dogs and vultures, that would be its final bath. {#p8 5.141}

> I pray to thee, O bull of the Kshatriya race, let not the Kshatriyas, old in learning and old in years, perish miserably, O Janardana, for thy sake. {#q5 5.141}
> — Karna

Let them die by weapons, he said, on Kurukshetra, the holiest place in the three worlds, so that they would all go to heaven. Bring Arjuna to face him, and keep their talk a secret for ever. {#p9 5.141}
