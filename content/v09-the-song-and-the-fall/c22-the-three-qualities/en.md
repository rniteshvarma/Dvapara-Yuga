Great Brahma is a womb for him, [Krishna](krishna) said; he places the seed in it, and from that all beings are born. Of every form born in any womb, Brahma is the great womb and he the father who gives the seed. {#p1 6.38}

Goodness, passion and darkness, the three qualities born of nature, bind the eternal embodied one to the body. Goodness, clear and free from pain, binds by attachment to happiness and knowledge. Passion, born of thirst and clinging, binds by attachment to work. Darkness, born of ignorance, binds by error, idleness and sleep. Now one rises above the other two, now another. {#p2 6.38}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna light=krishna}

When the light of knowledge shines through every gate of the body, goodness is strong. Greed, restlessness and craving show that passion has grown; gloom, idleness, error and delusion, that darkness has. A man who dies in goodness goes to the pure worlds of the wise; in passion, he is born among those attached to work; in darkness, among the ignorant. {#p3 6.38}

> They that dwell in goodness go on high; they that are addicted to passion dwell in the middle; (while) they that are of darkness, being addicted to the lowest quality, go down. {#q1 6.38}
> — Krishna

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

How is a man known, [Arjuna](arjuna) asked, who has gone beyond the three qualities? How does he live? {#p4 6.38}

He neither hates light, activity and delusion when they come nor longs for them when they go, Krishna said; he sits unconcerned, unshaken, knowing *it is only the qualities moving*. Pain and pleasure are the same to him; {#p5 6.38}

> to whom a sod of earth, a stone, and gold are alike. {#q2 6.38}
> — Krishna

Praise and blame, honour and dishonour, friend and foe are alike to him. And whoever serves Krishna with undivided devotion goes beyond the qualities too, for Krishna is the ground of Brahman, of the immortal, of eternal right and of unbroken joy. {#p6 6.38}
