Duryodhana gave the word, and the Kurus rushed in. [Bhishma](bhishma) poured arrows on Abhimanyu, Arjuna, the Kekayas, Virata and Dhrishtadyumna, and the heron array wavered; the Pandava chariot-divisions began to flee. Take me to the grandsire, [Arjuna](arjuna) told [Krishna](krishna); he will destroy my army for Duryodhana's sake. *Be careful*, said Krishna, and drove the great chariot with its roaring ape banner before Bhishma's. {#p1 6.52}

Bhishma, Drona, Kripa, Duryodhana, Shalya, Ashwatthama, Vikarna, Jayadratha and Shakuni all struck at Arjuna together, and he did not waver, like a mountain; he struck them all back. Satyaki, Virata, Dhrishtadyumna, the sons of Draupadi and Abhimanyu gathered around him. {#p2 6.52}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=duryodhana,bhishma light=duryodhana}

As Arjuna cut through the Kuru lines, [Duryodhana](duryodhana) came to Bhishma. {#p3 6.52}

> This mighty son of Pandu, O sire, accompanied by Krishna, felling all our troops, cutteth down our roots, even though thou, O son of Ganga, and that foremost of car-warriors, Drona, are alive. {#q1 6.52}
> — Duryodhana

It was for Bhishma's sake that Karna had laid down his arms, he said; let him kill Phalguni. Bhishma said *Fie on Kshatriya usage*, and drove at Arjuna's chariot. {#p4 6.52}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhishma,arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

The two white-horsed chariots faced each other, and both armies roared. Arjuna wrapped Bhishma in a thousand arrows, and Bhishma met them with a net of his own; flight after flight broke against flight. Then Bhishma struck Krishna between the breasts with three arrows. {#p5 6.52}

> And the slayer of Madhu, struck with those shafts shot from Bhishma's bow, shone in that battle, O king, like a flowering Kinsuka. {#q2 6.52}
> — Sanjaya

Arjuna, angered, struck Bhishma's charioteer. The two charioteers wheeled and circled, looking for an opening, and neither warrior could find a flaw in the other; at times both vanished in clouds of arrows. The gods, Gandharvas and sages watched and said to one another: {#p6 6.52}

> Indeed, a battle such as this will never take place again. {#q3 6.52}
> — the gods

Neither could beat the other, they said, as long as the world lasted. And all around them the armies cut each other down with swords and axes, and Drona and Dhrishtadyumna met. {#p7 6.52}
