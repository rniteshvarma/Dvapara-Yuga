What of those, [Arjuna](arjuna) asked, who set the scriptures aside and yet sacrifice with faith: is theirs goodness, passion or darkness? {#p1 6.41}

Faith is of three kinds, [Krishna](krishna) said, according to each one's nature. {#p2 6.41}

> A being here is full of faith; and whatever is one's faith, one is even that. {#q1 6.41}
> — Krishna

The good worship the gods; the passionate, Yakshas and Rakshasas; the dark, ghosts and spirits. And those who torture their bodies with fierce austerities the scriptures never ordained, full of hypocrisy and pride, torment the elements of their bodies and Krishna who dwells within: their resolve is demonic. {#p3 6.41}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna light=krishna}

Food too is of three kinds. Food that lengthens life and gives strength, health and joy, savoury and nourishing, pleases the good; bitter, sour, salty, over-hot, pungent, dry and burning food that brings pain and sickness pleases the passionate; stale, tasteless, stinking, spoiled food, leftovers and refuse, pleases the dark. {#p4 6.41}

So with sacrifice: done as a duty without longing for its fruit, it is good; done for reward or for show, passionate; done against the rule, with no food given, no mantras, no fees and no faith, dark. There is an austerity of the body, in reverence, purity, uprightness, chastity and harmlessness; and {#p5 6.41}

> The speech which causeth no agitation, which is true, which is agreeable and beneficial, and the diligent study of the Vedas, are said to be the penance of speech. {#q2 6.41}
> — Krishna

and an austerity of the mind, in serenity, gentleness, silence, self-control and purity. And a gift given because it should be given, to one who cannot repay it, at the right time and place, is good; given grudgingly or for return, passionate; given at the wrong place and time, to the unworthy, with contempt, dark. {#p6 6.41}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,arjuna light=krishna}

> OM, TAT, SAT, this is said to be the three-fold designation of Brahma. {#q3 6.41}
> — Krishna

By it the Brahmanas, the Vedas and sacrifice were ordained of old. So the knowers of Brahman begin every sacrifice, gift and austerity with *Om*; with *Tat* those who seek freedom do them without looking for the fruit; and *Sat* means what is real and good, and every steady act done for That. But whatever is offered, given or done without faith is *asat*, nothing, here or hereafter. {#p7 6.41}
