::scene{#s1 frame=bhishma,yudhishthira,bhima,nakula,sahadeva light=bhishma}

So [Bhishma](bhishma) counted the Pandavas. [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) was a great Ratha; [Bhima](bhima) was equal to eight; the twins were Rathas, beautiful as the Ashvins. All of them were half a cubit taller than other men; as children they had beaten Duryodhana's brothers at running, at aiming, at eating and at playing in the dust. Remembering Draupadi, they would range the field like Rudras. {#p1 5.170}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

And [Arjuna](arjuna), with Narayana beside him, had no equal among gods, Asuras or men, before or since: the ape on his banner, Vasudeva at the reins, Gandiva, the inexhaustible quivers, the weapons of Indra, Rudra, Kubera, Yama and Varuna. {#p2 5.170}

> Myself and preceptor (Drona) among the two armies, and no third car-warrior, O great king, can advance against Dhananjaya, that scatterer of arrowy showers! {#q1 5.170}
> — Bhishma

> He is skilful and young, while both of us are old and worn out! {#q2 5.170}
> — Bhishma

The kings, hearing it, thought of the Pandavas' valour as if it stood before them, and their great arms, smeared with sandal and hung with bracelets, *seemed to hang down divested of might*. {#p3 5.170}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=abhimanyu,satyaki,virata,drupada,uttar light=abhimanyu}

The five sons of Draupadi were Maharathas; Virata's son [Uttara](uttar) a foremost Ratha; [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu) a leader of leaders, equal to Arjuna or Vasudeva; [Satyaki](satyaki) the same, dauntless and very angry. Old [Virata](virata) and old [Drupada](drupada) were Maharathas, and their love for the Pandavas would make them stronger still. {#p4 5.171}

[Dhrishtadyumna](dhrishtadyumna) was an Atiratha; his young son Kshatradharma was only half a Ratha. Dhrishtaketu of Chedi, Chekitana, the five Kekaya brothers with their blood-red banners, King Pandya, Satyajit of Panchala: one after another Bhishma named them. [Kuntibhoja](kuntibhoja), Bhima's uncle, was an Atiratha; [Ghatotkacha](ghatotkacha), son of Bhima and Hidimba, a leader of leaders, full of illusions. {#p5 5.172 5.173}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=bhishma,shikhandi light=shikhandi}

Bhishma would face them all, he said, Arjuna and Vasudeva too, like the sun and the moon seen together at evening. All but one. [Shikhandi](shikhandi), whom he had named a foremost Ratha, he would not strike or kill even if he came at him with weapons raised. {#p6 5.172 5.173}

> Having notified my god-like vow among all the kings of the earth, I shall never slay a woman or one that was formerly a woman. {#q3 5.173}
> — Bhishma

Shikhandi had been born a daughter and had become a man. And the sons of Kunti, Bhishma said again, he would not be able to kill. {#p7 5.173}
