::scene{#s1 frame=krishna,balarama,pradyumna,samba,satyaki,yudhishthira,nakula,sahadeva light=krishna,yudhishthira}

When [Krishna](krishna) heard that Arjuna was home, he came to Indraprastha with [Balarama](balarama) and all the great men of the Vrishnis and Andhakas, his brothers and sons, with an army and with wedding gifts. [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) sent the twins to meet them. The streets were swept and watered and strewn with flowers and sprinkled with sandal-water, and the city was full of the scent of aloes. Yudhishthira embraced Krishna and smelt his head, and received every one of the Vrishni lords as they deserved. {#p1 1.223}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,balarama,subhadra,arjuna light=krishna,balarama}

Then Krishna gave Subhadra's dowry: a thousand golden chariots hung with bells, ten thousand milch cows of Mathura, a thousand white mares in golden harness, a thousand swift white mules, a thousand serving-girls, a great host of draught horses from the Bahlika country, and ten loads of gold for Subhadra herself. Balarama gave Arjuna a thousand war elephants like mountain peaks. That wave of wealth, the epic says, flowed into the Pandava ocean and filled it to the brim, to the sorrow of all their foes. {#p2 1.223}

The Kurus and the Vrishnis feasted and played together for many days. Then the Vrishnis went home to Dvaraka, with Balarama at their head. But Krishna stayed on with Arjuna, hunting deer and boar along the Yamuna. {#p3 1.223}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=subhadra,abhimanyu,arjuna,krishna light=abhimanyu}

Then Subhadra gave birth to a son, long-armed and broad-chested, with eyes like a bull's. They named him [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu), because he was fearless and full of fire. Yudhishthira gave ten thousand cows and gold to the Brahmanas. Krishna himself performed the rites of the child's infancy. {#p4 1.223}

He grew up like the waxing moon, the darling of his father and his uncles and of Krishna. He learnt the Vedas, and from his father every weapon, celestial and human, with every way of countering them, and quickness forward, backward, sideways and wheeling. Arjuna looked at him, the epic says, as Indra had looked at Arjuna. {#p5 1.223}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=draupadi,prativindhya,sutasoma,shrutakarma,shatanika,shrutasena,dhaumya light=draupadi,prativindhya,sutasoma,shrutakarma,shatanika,shrutasena}

[Draupadi](draupadi) bore five sons, one a year, one to each of her husbands. To Yudhishthira, [Prativindhya](prativindhya), who would stand against enemy weapons like the Vindhya hills. To Bhima, [Sutasoma](sutasoma), born after Bhima had performed a thousand Soma sacrifices. To Arjuna, [Shrutakarma](shrutakarma), born after his famous deeds in exile. To Nakula, [Shatanika](shatanika), named after a royal sage of the Kurus. To Sahadeva, [Shrutasena](shrutasena), born under the stars of Krittika. {#p6 1.223}

[Dhaumya](dhaumya) performed all their rites. They studied the Vedas, learnt every weapon from Arjuna, and loved one another; and the Pandavas, with sons like the children of the gods, were filled with joy. {#p7 1.223}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=1.223}
**Six boys at Indraprastha.** Abhimanyu and the five sons of Draupadi grow up together here, in the happiest years the Pandavas will have. None of the six will outlive the war.
::
