::scene{#s1 frame=arjuna,shiva light=arjuna}

Shiva vanished as the sun sets, and [Arjuna](arjuna) stood amazed. *I have both beheld and touched with my hand the three-eyed Hara.* His enemies, he felt, were already beaten. {#p1 3.41}

Then the guardians of the world came to him. Varuna, lord of the waters, blue as lapis lazuli, came with the rivers and the serpents. Kubera, lord of wealth, came golden on his chariot with his Yakshas. Yama, lord of the dead, came with the ancestors, mace in hand. And [Indra](indra) came last, on his white elephant Airavata, with his queen beside him. They stood on the summits around him. {#p2 3.41}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,bhishma,drona,karna,surya light=arjuna}

Yama spoke first, from the southern peak. Arjuna had been the sage Nara in his former life, he said, and had been born among men at Brahma's command. He would defeat Bhishma, and Drona's armies, and the demons called Nivatakavachas. {#p3 3.41}

> And, O son of the Kuru race, O Dhananjaya, thou shalt also slay Karna of fierce prowess, who is even a portion of my father Surya. {#q1 3.41}
> — Yama

And Yama gave him his own mace-weapon, which no one can turn aside. {#p4 3.41}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=3.41}
**A secret, told in heaven.** The god of death names Karna as a part of the Sun, his own father. No one on earth knows Karna is Kunti's son. Here in the mountains, a god says aloud half of what Kunti has kept hidden all her life.
::

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,indra light=arjuna}

Varuna, from the west, gave him his nooses, with which the gods had once bound a thousand demons; *even if Yama himself be thy foe … he will not be able to escape*. Kubera, from Kailasa, said that meeting Arjuna gave him as much joy as meeting Krishna, and gave him the weapon called Antardhana, which puts the enemy to sleep, the one Shiva used to burn the Tripura demons. {#p5 3.41}

Indra spoke last. Arjuna must come up to heaven, he said, and there he would be given all of Indra's weapons. His own chariot would come for him, with Matali at the reins. {#p6 3.41}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=arjuna,indra light=arjuna}

Soon the chariot came down through the clouds, roaring like a storm, drawn by ten thousand golden horses, loaded with swords and thunderbolts, flying the emerald banner Vaijayanta. *O lucky son of Sakra!* said Matali. Indra wished to see him. {#p7 3.42}

Arjuna bathed in the Ganga, offered water to his ancestors, and said goodbye to the mountain as a child leaves its father's lap: *every day have I lived happily on thy tablelands.* Then he climbed into the chariot and rose. {#p8 3.42}

***

::scene{#s5 frame=arjuna light=arjuna}

Above the earth there was no sun or moon or fire, only a light of its own. He saw thousands of chariots, and great shining worlds, each in its place, and asked Matali what they were. {#p9 3.42}

> These, O son of Pritha, are virtuous persons stationed in their respective places. It is these whom thou hast seen, O exalted one, as stars, from the earth. {#q2 3.42}
> — Matali

Past Airavata at the gate, through the worlds set apart for virtuous kings, Arjuna came at last to Amaravati, the city of Indra. {#p10 3.42}
