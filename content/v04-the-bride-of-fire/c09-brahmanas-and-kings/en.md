::scene{#s1 frame=drupada,duryodhana,karna,shalya light=drupada}

When [Drupada](drupada) made it known that he would give his daughter to the Brahmana who had hit the mark, the kings looked at one another and grew angry. {#p1 1.191}

> Passing us by and treating the assembled monarchs as straw this Drupada desireth to bestow his daughter—that first of women,—on a Brahmana! {#q1 1.191}
> — the kings

The svayamvara was for Kshatriyas, they said, not Brahmanas. He had planted a tree and was cutting it down just as it bore fruit. Kill him, and his son. And if the girl would not choose one of them, throw her into the fire and go home. The Brahmana himself they would spare, since a Brahmana must not be killed; but something had to be done so that no svayamvara ever ended like this again. {#p2 1.191}

They took up their weapons and rushed at Drupada, and Drupada, afraid, turned to the Brahmanas for protection. {#p3 1.191}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhima,arjuna,krishna,balarama light=bhima,arjuna}

[Bhima](bhima) and [Arjuna](arjuna) stepped out to meet the kings. Bhima tore up a large tree like an elephant, stripped its leaves, and stood beside his brother holding it, like Yama with his mace. Arjuna stood ready with his bow. {#p4 1.191}

[Krishna](krishna), watching, was sure now. {#p5 1.191}

> That hero there, of tread like that of a mighty lion, who draweth the large bow in his hand four full cubits in length, is Arjuna! There is no doubt, O Sankarshana, about this, if I am Vasudeva. {#q2 1.191}
> — Krishna

And the one with the tree was Bhima, he said; no one else alive could do that. The one who had left, with the lotus eyes, was Yudhishthira; and the two like Kartikeya must be the sons of the Ashvins. He had heard they had escaped the house of lac with their mother. [Balarama](balarama) was delighted that their father's sister Pritha was alive. {#p6 1.191}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,karna light=arjuna,karna}

The Brahmanas in the crowd shook their deer-skins and water-pots and shouted that they would fight too. Arjuna laughed and told them to stand aside and watch. {#p7 1.192}

[Karna](karna) came at Arjuna. Arjuna's arrows made him faint, and he came back more carefully. They fought until the arrows of each hid the other from sight, calling out *Behold the strength of my arms!* and *Mark how I have countered that!*, words, the epic says, that only heroes understand. {#p8 1.192}

> Art thou the embodiment of the science of weapons, or art thou Rama that best of Brahmanas, or Indra himself, or Indra's younger brother Vishnu? {#q3 1.192}
> — Karna

No one but Indra or Arjuna himself, Karna said, could fight him like this when he was angry. {#p9 1.192}

> O Karna, I am neither the science of arms (personified), nor Rama endued with superhuman powers. I am only a Brahmana who is the foremost of all warriors and all wielders of weapons. {#q4 1.192}
> — Arjuna

Karna stopped fighting, the epic says, because he thought a Brahmana's power could not be beaten. {#p10 1.192}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=1.192,1.138}
**The second meeting.** In the tournament at Hastinapura, the sun went down before Karna and Arjuna could fight. Here they fight at last, and neither knows the other: Arjuna is in disguise, and Karna does not know he is fighting his own brother.
::

***

::scene{#s4 frame=bhima,shalya light=bhima,shalya}

On another part of the field, Bhima and [Shalya](shalya) fought like two elephants in rut, with fists and knees, pushing and dragging and throwing each other down. Then Bhima lifted Shalya in his arms and threw him, without hurting him much. {#p11 1.192}

With Shalya down and Karna afraid, the kings grew uneasy. Who were these Brahmanas? Only Parashurama, Drona or Arjuna could stand against Karna; only Balarama, Bhima or Duryodhana could throw Shalya. Brahmanas must be protected anyway. Let them first find out who these men were. {#p12 1.192}

***

::scene{#s5 frame=krishna,bhima,arjuna,draupadi light=krishna}

Krishna, who had seen what Bhima did and was certain now, spoke to the kings gently: *This maiden hath been justly acquired.* And they gave up the fight and went home wondering, saying that the svayamvara had ended in a victory for the Brahmanas. {#p13 1.192}

Bhima and Arjuna pushed their way out of the crowd with difficulty, bruised, surrounded by Brahmanas in deer-skins, with Draupadi following them, like the sun and the moon coming out of the clouds. {#p14 1.192}

***

::scene{#s6 frame=kunti light=kunti}

At the potter's house, [Kunti](kunti) was frightened. Her sons were late back from begging. Had the sons of Dhritarashtra found them? Had Rakshasas killed them? Had Vyasa himself, who sent them here, been wrong? {#p15 1.192}

Then, in the stillness of the late afternoon, Arjuna came in through the door, with a crowd of Brahmanas, like the sun on a cloudy day. {#p16 1.192}
