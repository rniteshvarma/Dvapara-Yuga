::scene{#s1 frame=krishna,duryodhana,karna,shakuni,duhshasana light=duryodhana}

[Duryodhana](duryodhana) sat on his throne among a thousand kings, with [Duhshasana](duhshasana), [Karna](karna) and [Shakuni](shakuni) beside him. He rose to honour [Krishna](krishna), gave him the cow, honey, curds and water, and offered him palaces and the whole kingdom, and asked him to dine. Krishna would not. Why? asked Duryodhana, glancing at Karna. {#p1 5.91}

> Envoys, O king, eat and accept worship only after the success of their missions. {#q1 5.91}
> — Krishna

Duryodhana pressed him: they had no quarrel with Krishna, no war. {#p2 5.91}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,duryodhana light=krishna}

> Not from desire, nor from wrath, nor from malice, nor for gain, nor for the sake of argument, nor from temptation, would I abandon virtue. {#q2 5.91}
> — Krishna

One eats another's food out of love or in distress, said Krishna; Duryodhana had inspired no love in him, and he was not in distress. And Duryodhana had hated his gentle cousins from the day they were born. {#p3 5.91}

> He that hateth them, hateth me; he that loveth them, loveth me. Know that the virtuous Pandavas and my own self have but a common soul. {#q3 5.91}
> — Krishna

*Defiled by wickedness, all this food, therefore, deserveth not to be eaten by me. The food supplied by Vidura alone, should, I think, be eaten by me.* {#p4 5.91}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,vidura,kunti light=vidura}

He went to [Vidura](vidura)'s house. Drona, Kripa, Bhishma, Bahlika and many Kurus followed to offer him their houses; he thanked them and sent them away. Vidura entertained him, and [Kunti](kunti) set before him clean, savoury food. Krishna first gave a share, with wealth, to Brahmanas versed in the Vedas, and then ate what remained with his men. {#p5 5.91}

::aside{#a1 kind=versions src=5.91}
The popular story of Vidura's wife, so overjoyed that she fed Krishna the banana peels and threw away the fruit, is a later devotional tale. In the epic, Vidura entertains Krishna and it is Kunti who serves the food.
::
