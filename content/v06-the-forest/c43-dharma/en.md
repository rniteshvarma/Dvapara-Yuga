The brothers stood up, and their hunger and thirst were gone. *I cannot take thee for a Yaksha!* said [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) to the being standing on one leg in the lake. Was he one of the Vasus, or Rudras, or Indra himself? *Art thou a friend of ours, or even our father himself?* {#p1 3.312}

> I am even thy father, the Lord of justice, possessed of great prowess! Know, bull of the Bharata race, that I came hither desirous of beholding thee! {#q1 3.312}
> — Dharma

Fame, truth, self-restraint, purity, modesty, charity and austerity were his body, said [Dharma](dharma), and harmlessness and peace were the doors to him. He had come to test his son, and was pleased with him. Ask a boon. {#p2 3.312}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=dharma,yudhishthira light=dharma}

Yudhishthira's first boon was for the Brahmana: let his sacred fire not go out. *It was I*, said Dharma, *who … in the guise of a deer, was carrying away that Brahmana's fire-sticks.* {#p3 3.312}

For his second, Yudhishthira asked that in the thirteenth year, now come, no one should recognise them. {#p4 3.312}

> Ye perpetuators of the Kuru race, through my grace, ye will spend this thirteenth year, secretly and unrecognised, in Virata's kingdom! {#q2 3.312}
> — Dharma

Ask a third, said Dharma. *Thou … art born of me, and Vidura of portion of mine!* {#p5 3.312}

> May I, O lord, always conquer covetousness and folly and anger, and may my mind be ever devoted to charity, truth, and ascetic austerities! {#q3 3.312}
> — Yudhishthira

You have those already by nature, said Dharma, and vanished. The brothers went back to the hermitage and gave the Brahmana his fire-sticks. {#p6 3.312}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=3.312}
**Vidura, too.** In one sentence Dharma confirms what the Adi Parva told: that Vidura was born of a portion of Dharma, cursed to be born a man. Yudhishthira and Vidura are, in this sense, kin twice over.
::

***

::scene{#s3 frame=yudhishthira,dhaumya,bhima light=yudhishthira}

Then the Pandavas told the sages who had shared their exile that they must now go into hiding for the last year, and Yudhishthira, his voice choked with tears, fainted. [Dhaumya](dhaumya) reminded him that the gods themselves had lived in disguise to beat their enemies: Indra in Nishadha, Vishnu as a dwarf to take Bali's kingdom, as a man in Dasharatha's house to kill Ravana. {#p7 3.313}

Bhima said Arjuna had never acted rashly without his brother's leave, and Bhima had always held back the twins. *Never shall we swerve from that in which thou wilt engage us!* {#p8 3.313}

So the Brahmanas blessed them and went home, and the five Pandavas and Draupadi set out with Dhaumya, walked two miles, and sat down to plan the year in hiding. {#p9 3.313}

And so ended twelve years in the forest. {#p10 3.313}
