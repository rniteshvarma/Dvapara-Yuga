[Ashwatthama](ashwatthama) came like a hurricane, and the arrows between them hid the sun and hushed the wind. While [Arjuna](arjuna) ranged the field, Drona's son found his moment and cut the string of the Gandiva with a horseshoe-headed arrow. The gods applauded, and so did Drona, Bhishma, Karna and Kripa. Then Ashwatthama pierced Arjuna in the chest. {#p1 4.59}

> Thereupon, with a loud laughter, the mighty-armed son of Pritha attached a strong and fresh string to Gandiva. {#q1 4.59}

He wet the new string with the sweat of his brow and came on again *as an infuriated leader of a herd of elephants rusheth at another elephant*. Arjuna's two quivers never emptied; Ashwatthama's did. That was how Arjuna prevailed. {#p2 4.59}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,karna light=karna}

Then a great bow twanged, and Arjuna turned and saw [Karna](karna). He left Drona's son and rushed at him. {#p3 4.59}

> The time, O Karna, hath now come for making good thy loquacious boast in the midst of the assembly, viz., that there is none equal to thee in fight. {#q2 4.60}
> — Arjuna

Karna had watched Panchala's princess outraged in the court, Arjuna said; now let him reap it. Karna answered that Arjuna's words always ran ahead of his deeds; that his forbearance at the dice had been weakness; that thirteen years of an ascetic's life had left him feeble. {#p4 4.60}

> Even now, O Radha's son, thou hadst fled from battle with me, and it is for this that thou livest although thy younger brother hath been slain. {#q3 4.60}
> — Arjuna

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,karna,uttar light=arjuna}

Their volleys pierced horses, arms and finger-guards. Arjuna cut the straps of Karna's quiver; Karna struck Arjuna's hand so that his grip on the bow loosened. Arjuna broke Karna's bow; Karna hurled a dart, and Arjuna cut it down. Karna's followers rushed in and Arjuna sent them to Yama's house. He killed Karna's horses, then drove a blazing arrow through his mail into his chest. Karna's sight went dark. When he came to himself, in great pain, he left the field and fled north, while Arjuna and [Uttara](uttar) shouted insults after him. {#p5 4.60}
