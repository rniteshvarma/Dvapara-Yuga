::scene{#s1 frame=arjuna,satyaki,abhimanyu light=arjuna}

The kings surrounded [Arjuna](arjuna) with thousands of chariots and hurled lances, maces, darts and axes at him like a flight of locusts, and he cut them all down with his golden arrows, while gods, Danavas, Gandharvas and Rakshasas cried *Excellent!* Shakuni's Gandharas smashed [Satyaki](satyaki)'s chariot, and he leapt onto [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu)'s, and the two of them on one chariot cut through Shakuni's army *like the sun and the moon together in the sky*. {#p1 6.58}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=bhima,ghatotkacha,duryodhana light=bhima}

[Bhima](bhima) and [Ghatotkacha](ghatotkacha) did great deeds, Ghatotkacha *transcending his very father*. When [Duryodhana](duryodhana) came against them, Bhima, smiling, shot him in the chest; Duryodhana sank down on his chariot in a faint, and his charioteer carried him off. His troops broke and fled, Bhima pursuing them, and Bhishma and Drona could not stop the flight. {#p2 6.58}

When Duryodhana came to, he rallied them himself, and the warriors stopped wherever they saw him, from shame. Then he went to [Bhishma](bhishma). {#p3 6.58}

> Without doubt, O grandsire, the sons of Pandu are being favoured by thee, inasmuch as thou forgivest, O hero, this slaughter of my army. {#q1 6.58}
> — Duryodhana

He should have been told before the war, he said, that Bhishma and Drona would not fight the Pandavas; then he would have taken counsel with Karna. If they did not mean to abandon him, let them fight with all their strength. {#p4 6.58}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhishma,duryodhana light=bhishma}

Bhishma laughed again and again, his eyes rolling with anger. He had told Duryodhana many times what was for his good: the Pandavas could not be beaten by the gods themselves. But what an old man could do, he would do. {#p5 6.58}

> Today, in the very sight of all, alone I shall check the sons of Pandu at the head of their troops and with all their kinsfolk. {#q2 6.58}
> — Bhishma

Duryodhana, delighted, had the conchs blown and the drums beaten, and the Pandavas answered with their own. {#p6 6.58}
