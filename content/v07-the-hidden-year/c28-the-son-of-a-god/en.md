[Uttara](uttar) came in, touched his father's feet, and saw Kanka sitting on the ground at the end of the hall, bleeding, with the Sairindhri tending him. Who had done this? {#p1 4.68}

> This crooked Brahmana hath been struck by me. He deserveth even more than this. When I was praising thee, he praised that person of the third sex. {#q1 4.68}
> — Virata

> Thou hast, O king, committed an improper act. Do thou speedily propitiate him so that the virulent poison of a Brahmana's curse may not consume thee to thy roots! {#q2 4.68}
> — Uttara

So [Virata](virata) begged Kanka's pardon. {#p2 4.68}

> O king, I have long ago forgiven it. Anger I have none. Had this blood from my nostrils fallen on the ground, then, without doubt, thou, O monarch, wouldst have been destroyed with thy kingdom. {#q3 4.68}
> — Yudhishthira

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,virata,uttar light=arjuna}

When the bleeding had stopped, Brihannala came in, saluted Virata and Kanka, and stood silent. And in [Arjuna](arjuna)'s hearing the king praised his son. How had he faced Karna, who never missed a mark among a thousand? Bhishma, who had no equal among men? Drona, the teacher of all Kshatriyas? Ashwatthama, Duryodhana? *Without doubt, thou, O bull amongst men, has routed the foe.* {#p3 4.68}

> The kine have not been recovered by me, nor have the foe been vanquished by me. All that hath been accomplished by the son of a deity. {#q4 4.69}
> — Uttara

A youth like a god had stopped him as he ran, he said, mounted his chariot, driven back Kripa and Drona and his son, Karna and Bhishma, and taken their clothes. *Beholding all this, venerable sire, my thighs began to quake.* Where was this youth? asked the king; he wanted to honour him. {#p4 4.69}

> The mighty son of a deity disappeared there and then. I think, however, that he will show himself either tomorrow or the day after. {#q5 4.69}
> — Uttara

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,uttara_f light=uttara_f}

And Virata still did not know him. With the king's leave, Arjuna gave the garments he had brought, with his own hands, to Virata's daughter, and the princess [Uttara](uttara_f) was delighted with the costly new clothes. {#p5 4.69}
