In the morning [Duryodhana](duryodhana) asked his commanders before all the troops: in how many days could each of them destroy the Pandava army? [Bhishma](bhishma) said ordinary men should be fought plainly and deceivers with deceit. Taking ten thousand soldiers and a thousand chariot-warriors a day, he could destroy it; or, using his great weapons, {#p1 5.196}

> then I can, O Bharata, finish the slaughter in a month. {#q1 5.196}
> — Bhishma

***

::scene{#s2 frame=drona,kripa,ashwatthama,karna light=karna}

[Drona](drona) smiled. {#p2 5.196}

> I am old, O mighty-armed one! My energy and activity have both become weak. {#q2 5.196}
> — Drona

Still, he could do it in a month, like Bhishma. [Kripa](kripa) said two months. [Ashwatthama](ashwatthama) said ten nights. [Karna](karna) said five days. And Bhishma laughed aloud: so Karna might think, as long as he had not yet met Arjuna in battle with Vasudeva beside him. {#p3 5.196}

> Why, thou art capable of saying anything, even what thou pleasest! {#q3 5.196}
> — Bhishma

***

::scene{#s3 frame=yudhishthira,arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

[Yudhishthira](yudhishthira)'s spies brought him all of this by morning, and he called his brothers and asked [Arjuna](arjuna) the same question. Arjuna looked at Krishna. These were great warriors, he said, and could no doubt do as they said; but let the king's anxiety go. With Vasudeva beside him, on a single chariot, he could destroy the three worlds in the twinkling of an eye: he still had the terrible weapon Shiva gave him when they fought hand to hand, the hunter and the archer, the weapon Shiva uses at the end of an age. None of them knew it, not Bhishma, nor Drona, nor Kripa, nor Ashwatthama, let alone Karna. {#p4 5.197}

> It is not, however, proper to slay ordinary men in battle by means of celestial weapons. We shall (on the other hand) vanquish our foes in a fair fight. {#q4 5.197}
> — Arjuna

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=5.197}
This is the Pashupata weapon, which Arjuna won from Shiva in the guise of a Kirata hunter during the forest years; Volume VI, chapter 10 tells that fight.
::

And they had allies enough, he said: Shikhandi, Satyaki, Dhrishtadyumna, Bhima and the twins, Virata and Drupada, Ghatotkacha and his son Anjanaparvan, Abhimanyu and the sons of Draupadi. And Yudhishthira himself: whoever he looked on in anger was sure to die. {#p5 5.197}
