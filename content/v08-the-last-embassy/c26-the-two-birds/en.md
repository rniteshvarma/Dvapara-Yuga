[Vidura](vidura) told a story. A fowler spread his net, and two birds who lived together were caught in it at once. They flew up with the net, and the fowler ran after them. An ascetic asked him why a creature of the earth chased creatures of the air. {#p1 5.64}

> These two, united together, are taking away my snare. There, however, where they will quarrel they will come under my control. {#q1 5.64}
> — the fowler

The birds quarrelled, fell, and the fowler took them both. So do kinsmen who fall out over wealth fall into the hands of their enemies. {#p2 5.64}

> Kinsmen, O Dhritarashtra, O bull of the Bharata race, are like charred brands, which blaze up when united but only smoke when disunited. {#q2 5.64}
> — Vidura

***

::scene{#s2 frame=vidura,duryodhana light=vidura}

Once, he said, on the mountain Gandhamadana, he had seen a jar of golden honey on an inaccessible cliff, Kubera's own drink, guarded by deadly snakes; whoever drank it would never die, the blind would see, the old grow young. The hunters with him went for it, and all of them died. {#p3 5.64}

> In the same way, this thy son desireth to enjoy the whole earth without a rival. He beholdeth the honey, but seeth not, from folly, the terrible fall. {#q3 5.64}
> — Vidura

*O Dhritarashtra, take king Yudhishthira on thy lap.* {#p4 5.64}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=dhritarashtra,duryodhana,krishna,arjuna light=dhritarashtra}

[Dhritarashtra](dhritarashtra) tried again. The five sons of Pandu were like the five elements upholding all things. And as for [Krishna](krishna): {#p5 5.65}

> As regards Krishna, his wives, kinsmen, relatives, his own soul and the whole earth, put on one scale, weigheth with Dhananjaya on the other. {#q4 5.65}
> — Dhritarashtra

Let him take the Pandavas by the hand as brothers. {#p6 5.65}

And what had [Arjuna](arjuna) said, after Krishna? He had named every king gathered for the Kurus, *on the eve of death*, offerings for the Pandava fire, and sent them this: {#p7 5.66}

> If, indeed, ye do not give up unto Yudhishthira, that slayer of foes, his own share in the kingdom asked back by him, I shall then, by means of my arrows, send all of you, with cavalry, infantry, and elephants, into the inauspicious regions of departed spirits. {#q5 5.66}
> — Arjuna
