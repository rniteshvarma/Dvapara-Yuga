After the rescue of Draupadi, the Pandavas left Kamyaka and went back to the Dvaita woods, living on fruit and keeping strict vows. There, at the very end of their exile, they had one last trouble, *which, however, was destined to bring about their future happiness*. {#p1 3.309}

A deer, rubbing itself against a tree, caught a Brahmana's fire-sticks and churning-staff in its antlers and bounded away with them. Without them he could not light his sacred fire. He came to [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira) in distress: track the deer and bring them back. {#p2 3.309}

The brothers armed themselves and chased it. They shot arrows and darts and javelins at it, and could not pierce it, and then it vanished. Tired, hungry and thirsty, they sat down in the shade of a banyan. {#p3 3.309}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=nakula,bhima,arjuna,sahadeva light=nakula}

> In our race, O king, virtue hath never been sacrificed, nor hath there been loss of wealth from insolence. And being asked, we have never said to any creature, Nay! Why then in the present case have we met with this disaster? {#q1 3.309}
> — Nakula

There is no limit to calamities, said Yudhishthira, and no knowing their cause. But each of the others had an answer. [Bhima](bhima): because I did not kill the usher who dragged Draupadi into the hall. [Arjuna](arjuna): because I let the Suta's son's words go. [Sahadeva](sahadeva): because I did not kill Shakuni when he beat you at dice. {#p4 3.310}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=nakula,sahadeva,arjuna,bhima light=nakula}

Yudhishthira sent [Nakula](nakula) up a tree to look for water. He saw trees that grow by water and heard cranes, and went to fetch some in the quivers. He found a crystal lake. As he bent to drink, a voice spoke from the sky. {#p5 3.310}

> O child, do not commit this rash act! This lake hath already been in my possession. {#q2 3.310}
> — the voice

*First answer my questions, and then drink.* But Nakula was too thirsty to listen. He drank, and fell down dead. {#p6 3.310}

Yudhishthira sent Sahadeva after him, and Sahadeva found his brother dead, heard the voice, drank, and died. Then Arjuna went, bow in hand, and saw them lying there as if asleep. *Do thou forbid me by appearing before me!* he cried, and shot arrows into the air at the sound of the voice, and drank, and died. Then Bhima went, thought a Yaksha or Rakshasa had done it, decided to drink first and fight after, and died. {#p7 3.310}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=yudhishthira light=yudhishthira}

At last Yudhishthira went himself, through a forest with no human sound in it, and came to a beautiful lake, as if built by the architect of the gods. And beside it lay his four brothers. {#p8 3.310}
