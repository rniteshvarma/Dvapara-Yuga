If understanding is better than action, [Arjuna](arjuna) asked, why push him into this terrible work? Krishna's words seemed to point two ways; let him say one thing plainly. {#p1 6.27}

There are two paths, [Krishna](krishna) answered: knowledge for the Sankhyas, action for the yogis. But no one becomes free of action by not acting, and no one can stay a moment without acting. The man who holds back his limbs while his mind dwells on their objects is a hypocrite; the man who works with his limbs while his mind holds them, without attachment, is the better. {#p2 6.27}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,arjuna light=krishna}

All work binds except work done as sacrifice. The Lord of creatures made men and sacrifice together: by sacrifice nourish the gods, and the gods will nourish you. He who enjoys their gifts without giving back is a thief. All creatures come from food, and sacrifice comes from work; work comes from the Vedas, and the Vedas from the Imperishable, so the all-pervading one is set in sacrifice. Whoever does not turn with this wheel lives in vain. {#p3 6.27}

Janaka and others reached perfection by work. And people follow the great. {#p4 6.27}

> There is nothing whatever for me, O Partha, to do in the three worlds, (since I have) nothing for me which hath not been acquired; still I engage in action. {#q1 6.27}
> — Krishna

If he stopped, the worlds would perish. So let the wise man work without attachment, as the ignorant work with it, to keep the world to its duties. It is the qualities of nature that act; only the man deluded by *I* thinks *I am the doer*. Give all your work to me, Krishna said, and fight, without desire, without fever. {#p5 6.27}

> One's own duty, even if imperfectly performed, is better than being done by other even if well performed. {#q2 6.27}
> — Krishna

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,krishna light=krishna}

Then what drives a man to sin, Arjuna asked, even against his will, as if by force? {#p6 6.27}

> It is desire, it is wrath, born of the attribute of passion; it is all devouring, it is very sinful. Know this to be the foe in this world. {#q3 6.27}
> — Krishna

As smoke covers fire, dust a mirror, the womb the unborn child, so desire covers knowledge. It lives in the senses, the mind and the understanding. The senses are higher than the body, the mind higher than the senses, understanding higher than the mind, and higher than understanding is He. Knowing that, steady the self by the self, and kill this enemy. {#p7 6.27}
