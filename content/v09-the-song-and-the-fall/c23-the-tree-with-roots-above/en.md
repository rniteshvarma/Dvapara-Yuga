> They say that the Aswattha, having its roots above and branches below, is eternal, its leaves are the Chhandas. He who knoweth it, knoweth the Vedas. {#q1 6.39}
> — Krishna

Its branches spread down and up, fed by the qualities, its buds the objects of the senses; below, its roots stretch down into the world of men, binding them to action. Here its form cannot be known, nor its end, its beginning or its ground. Cut it down, [Krishna](krishna) said, with the strong axe of detachment, and then seek that place from which no one returns, taking refuge in the first Being from whom this ancient stream of life flowed. {#p1 6.39}

> The sun lighteth not that [seat], nor the moon, nor fire. Whither going none returneth, that is my supreme seat. {#q2 6.39}
> — Krishna

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna light=krishna}

An eternal fragment of him becomes the living soul in the world, and draws to itself the five senses and the mind. When it takes a body or leaves one, it carries them with it as the wind carries scents from their flowers. The deluded do not see it come or go; those with the eye of knowledge see. {#p2 6.39}

The light in the sun that lights the world, and in the moon, and in fire, is his. He enters the earth and holds up all creatures, becomes the moon and nourishes the plants, becomes the fire of life in breathing bodies and digests their food. {#p3 6.39}

> I am seated in the hearts of all. From Me are memory and knowledge and the loss of both. {#q3 6.39}
> — Krishna

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,arjuna light=arjuna}

There are two beings in the world, the perishable and the imperishable: all creatures are perishable, and the unchanging one is called imperishable. But there is another, the highest Being, the Supreme Self, the eternal Lord who enters and sustains the three worlds; because he is beyond the perishable and higher even than the imperishable, he is called *Purushottama*, the highest Being. Whoever knows him so knows all, said Krishna to [Arjuna](arjuna), and has done all there is to do. {#p4 6.39}
