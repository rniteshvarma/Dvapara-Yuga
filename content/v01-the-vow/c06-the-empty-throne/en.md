::scene{#s1 frame=shantanu,satyavati,chitrangada_k,vichitravirya light=chitrangada_k,vichitravirya}

When the wedding was over, [Shantanu](shantanu) brought his bride home, and soon [Satyavati](satyavati) bore him a son. They named him [Chitrangada](chitrangada_k). He was clever and brave and full of energy, and he grew into a remarkable man. A second son followed, [Vichitravirya](vichitravirya), who would become a mighty bowman, and a king. {#p1 1.101}

So the fisher-chief had his wish. The throne of the Kurus would pass to Satyavati's sons, and [Bhishma](bhishma), who might have sat on it, stood guard beside it instead. {#p2 1.100 1.101}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=shantanu,bhishma,satyavati light=bhishma,satyavati}

But Shantanu did not live to see his younger son grown. Before Vichitravirya came of age, the wise king, as the text says, *realised the inevitable influence of Time*, and went to heaven. {#p3 1.101}

Bhishma kept his vow. He placed himself under Satyavati's command and crowned her elder son, Chitrangada. {#p4 1.101}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=chitrangada_k,bhishma light=chitrangada_k}

Chitrangada was a fierce king. He defeated every ruler who stood against him, and soon he thought no one, man or Asura or god, was his equal. {#p5 1.101}

A mortal who could defeat men, Asuras and even gods drew a challenger from the sky: the mighty king of the Gandharvas, who bore the same name, Chitrangada. The two met on the field of Kurukshetra, on the banks of the Saraswati, and fought for three whole years under a ceaseless rain of weapons. {#p6 1.101}

In the end the Gandharva, stronger or more skilled in deception, killed the Kuru king, and rose back into heaven. {#p7 1.101}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=1.101}
**A field to remember.** The plain where Chitrangada dies is the same field where, two generations later, the great war will be fought.
::

***

::scene{#s4 frame=bhishma,vichitravirya,satyavati light=vichitravirya,bhishma}

Bhishma performed his brother's funeral rites. Then he crowned the boy Vichitravirya, still too young to rule, as king of the Kurus. {#p8 1.101}

Vichitravirya placed himself under Bhishma's guidance and honoured him, and Bhishma, who knew every rule of law and duty, protected the young king who listened so well. {#p9 1.101}

::scene{#s5 frame=bhishma,amba,ambika,ambalika light=amba,ambika,ambalika}

For years Bhishma ruled for him, always at Satyavati's word. And when at last the young king came of age, Bhishma began to look for a bride for his brother. {#p10 1.102}
