::scene{#s1 frame=bhishma,drona,duryodhana light=bhishma}

When the night passed, [Bhishma](bhishma) went out angry at the head of the army, with [Drona](drona), [Duryodhana](duryodhana), Bahlika, Durmarshana, Chitrasena and Jayadratha around him like the gods around Indra. The banners on the elephants, red and yellow, black and brown, waved in front of the ranks, and the army rolled toward Arjuna *like the fierce current of the ocean-going Ganga*. {#p1 6.60}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

[Arjuna](arjuna) saw it from far off like a mass of clouds, and drove at its head with [Krishna](krishna) at the reins, and the Kurus were dismayed at the sight of the ape banner. His own array had four thousand elephants at each corner, like the one Yudhishthira had formed the day before. Thousands of drums and conchs sounded, bowstrings drowned the drums, and the dust spread over the sky like a canopy. {#p2 6.60}

Chariot-warriors fell with their charioteers and banners, elephants with elephants, horsemen with horsemen; shields studded with golden stars lay broken on the field; men were crushed under the rush of elephants in close ranks. {#p3 6.60}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhishma,arjuna,abhimanyu light=abhimanyu}

Then Bhishma, under his banner of five palm trees, caught sight of the ape banner and rushed at it, with Drona, Kripa, Shalya, Vivimshati, Duryodhana and Somadatta's son. [Abhimanyu](abhimanyu), in golden armour, came out of the ranks against all of them and turned their weapons aside, shining *like the adorable Agni himself, on the sacrificial altar*. {#p4 6.60}

Bhishma passed the boy by and went for Arjuna himself. Arjuna met his weapons with Gandiva, and Bhishma scattered Arjuna's arrows as the sun scatters the night. And the Kurus and the Srinjayas all stopped to watch the two of them fight. {#p5 6.60}
