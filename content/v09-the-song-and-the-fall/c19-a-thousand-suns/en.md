His delusion was gone, [Arjuna](arjuna) said. He believed it all. And now he wanted to see that sovereign form, if Krishna thought him able. {#p1 6.35}

You cannot see me with these eyes, [Krishna](krishna) said; I give you divine sight. And he showed it. {#p2 6.35}

> If the splendour of a thousand suns were to burst forth at once in the sky, (then) that would be like the splendour of that Mighty One. {#q1 6.35}
> — Sanjaya

Many mouths and eyes, many wonders, divine ornaments and raised weapons, garlands and robes, facing every way; and in that one body the whole universe, divided and subdivided. Arjuna's hair stood on end; he bowed his head and joined his hands. {#p3 6.35}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=arjuna,krishna,bhishma,drona,karna light=krishna}

He saw all the gods in that body, and Brahma on his lotus, and the sages and the serpents; arms, bellies, mouths and eyes without number, without beginning, middle or end; the sun and moon for eyes, a blazing fire for a mouth, heating the world. The three worlds trembled. Hosts of gods were entering him, some joining their hands in fear. {#p4 6.35}

And he saw Dhritarashtra's sons with all their kings, Bhishma, Drona and Karna, and the chief warriors of his own side too, rushing into those terrible mouths, some caught between the teeth with their heads crushed. {#p5 6.35}

> As moths with increasing speed rush for (their own) destruction to the blazing fire, so also do (these) people, with unceasing speed, enter thy mouths for (their) destruction. {#q2 6.35}
> — Arjuna

Who are you, Arjuna asked, in this terrible form? {#p6 6.35}

> I am Death, the destroyer of the worlds, fully developed. I am now engaged in slaying the race of men. {#q3 6.35}
> — Krishna

Even without Arjuna, Krishna said, none of these warriors would live. {#p7 6.35}

> By me have all these been already slain. Be only (my) instrument. {#q4 6.35}
> — Krishna

Kill Drona, Bhishma, Jayadratha and Karna, whom I have already killed; do not be afraid; fight, and you will win. {#p8 6.35}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

Trembling, his voice choked, Arjuna bowed again and praised him: the first god, the ancient being, Vayu and Yama, Agni and Varuna, the moon and Prajapati, *obeisance a thousand times, from before and from behind and from every side*. {#p9 6.35}

> Regarding (thee) a friend whatever hath been said by me carelessly, such as—O Krishna, O Yadava, O friend,—not knowing this thy greatness from want of judgement or from love either. {#q5 6.35}
> — Arjuna

For every joke at play, at rest, at meals, alone or in company, he begged forgiveness, as a father forgives a son, a friend a friend, a lover the beloved. He was glad and afraid. Show me again your other form, he said, the four-armed one with crown, mace and discus. {#p10 6.35}

No one but you has seen this form, Krishna said, not by Vedas or sacrifice, gifts or austerity; only by single-minded devotion can he be seen so. Then he took his own gentle shape again, and comforted the frightened man. *Now I am myself again*, said Arjuna. {#p11 6.35}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=6.35}
Krishna says here that no one but Arjuna has seen this form, yet the epic has already told of a universal form shown in the Kuru court (Volume VIII, chapter 47), seen there by Drona, Bhishma, Vidura, Sanjaya and the sages. The epic leaves the two side by side.
::
