That afternoon [Bhishma](bhishma) drove at the Pandavas with all of Duryodhana's brothers around him. Severed heads and ornamented arms twitched on the ground; headless men stood holding their bows; a river of blood ran toward the ocean of the next world. Bhishma, his bow always drawn to a circle, named each Pandava warrior before he killed him, and moved so fast along the track of his chariot that he seemed a wheel of fire. {#p1 6.59}

> the Pandavas in that battle, along with the Srinjayas, beheld that hero, though really alone, as multiplied a thousand-fold. {#q1 6.59}
> — Sanjaya

They saw him in the east and then in the west, in the north and then in the south; they saw only his arrows. Yudhishthira's army fled in a thousand directions, men throwing off their armour, their hair loose, like a herd of cattle. {#p2 6.59}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna,arjuna,bhishma light=krishna}

[Krishna](krishna) stopped the chariot. {#p3 6.59}

> The hour is now come, O Partha, which was desired by thee. Strike Bhishma, O tiger among men, else, thou wilt lose the senses. {#q2 6.59}
> — Krishna

Arjuna had sworn in the assembly of kings to kill Bhishma and Drona and all who fought him; let him keep his word. *Drive on to Bhishma*, said [Arjuna](arjuna). Bhishma covered the chariot in arrows until it vanished; Krishna, unafraid, drove on through them. Arjuna cut Bhishma's bow twice. {#p4 6.59}

> Excellent, O Partha, O thou of mighty arms, excellent, O son of Pandu. {#q3 6.59}
> — Bhishma

Then Bhishma pierced both Krishna and Arjuna all over until they looked like two bulls scored by horns, and laughed, and made Krishna tremble. And Krishna saw how hard Bhishma fought and how *mildly* Arjuna fought back, out of respect for his grandfather, and that the army could not survive it. {#p5 6.59}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,bhishma,arjuna,satyaki light=krishna}

[Satyaki](satyaki) came to Arjuna's side and shouted at the fleeing kings: *Kshatriyas, where are you going? This is not the duty of the righteous*. Let them go, Krishna told him. {#p6 6.59}

> Therefore, taking up my fierce discus, I will slay Bhishma of high vows. {#q4 6.59}
> — Krishna

He would kill Bhishma and Drona and all the sons of Dhritarashtra today, he said, and give Yudhishthira his kingdom. He dropped the reins, leapt down from the chariot, and ran at Bhishma with the discus Sudarshana whirling on his arm, sharp-edged and bright as the sun, the earth trembling under his tread and his yellow cloth streaming *like a cloud charged with lightning*. All creatures cried out, thinking the end of the Kurus had come. {#p7 6.59}

Bhishma did not move from his chariot. {#p8 6.59}

> Come, Come, O Lord of the gods, O thou that hast the universe for thy abode. {#q5 6.59}
> — Bhishma

> Slain here by thee, O Krishna, great will be my good fortune both in this world and the next. {#q6 6.59}
> — Bhishma

*You are the root of this slaughter*, Krishna said; a minister should restrain a king given to gambling. Destiny is all-powerful, Bhishma answered; he had told Dhritarashtra to abandon his son as the Yadavas abandoned Kamsa, and the king would not listen. {#p9 6.59}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=arjuna,krishna light=arjuna}

Then Arjuna jumped down and ran after Krishna and seized him by both arms; Krishna dragged him along *like a tempest bearing away a single tree*. Arjuna caught his legs and stopped him, with difficulty, at the tenth step. {#p10 6.59}

> Quell this wrath of thine. Thou art the refuge of the Pandavas, O Kesava. {#q7 6.59}
> — Arjuna

He swore by his sons and his brothers that he would not hold back from what he had pledged; at Krishna's command he would destroy the Kurus. Krishna was satisfied. He climbed back on the chariot, took up the reins again, and blew Panchajanya. {#p11 6.59}

::aside{#a1 kind=versions src=6.59}
Before the war Krishna had joined the Pandavas on condition that he would not fight (Volume VIII, chapter 2), and here he nearly breaks it. Many later retellings add that Bhishma had vowed to make Krishna take up arms; in Ganguli's text Bhishma's vow on this day is only to check the Pandavas himself, and the story of a vow about Krishna is not here.
::
