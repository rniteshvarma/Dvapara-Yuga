::scene{#s1 frame=yudhishthira,arjuna light=yudhishthira}

Twelve years were over, said [Yudhishthira](yudhishthira). The thirteenth, the hard one, had come. Where should they hide? {#p1 4.1}

[Arjuna](arjuna) named the kingdoms around the Kuru lands: Panchala, Chedi, Matsya, Surasena, Dasharna, Malla, Shalva, Saurashtra, Avanti. Yudhishthira chose Matsya: its old king [Virata](virata) was virtuous, generous and well liked, and friendly to the Pandavas. They would enter his service. But as what? {#p2 4.1}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=yudhishthira light=yudhishthira}

Yudhishthira would go as a Brahmana named Kanka, skilled at dice, and keep the king company at the board. {#p3 4.1}

> Formerly I was the bosom friend of Yudhishthira. {#q1 4.1}
> — Yudhishthira, rehearsing

***

::scene{#s3 frame=bhima light=bhima}

[Bhima](bhima) would be Vallabha, a cook: he would make curries better than any cook the king had, carry huge loads of wood, tame elephants and bulls, and wrestle in the lists, never killing anyone, only bringing them down. *Formerly I was the wrestler and cook of Yudhishthira.* {#p4 4.2}

***

::scene{#s4 frame=arjuna,urvashi light=arjuna}

And Arjuna? Yudhishthira praised him at length: the first of bowmen as the sun is first of lights. What could such a man hide as? {#p5 4.2}

> O lord of the Earth, I will declare myself as one of the neuter sex. {#q2 4.2}
> — Arjuna

The scars of the bowstring on his arms he would hide under bangles. He would wear earrings and conch-shell bracelets and a long braid, call himself Brihannala, and teach the women of the palace singing and dancing and music. {#p6 4.2}

::aside{#a1 kind=note src=4.2}
**Urvashi's curse, used.** This is the year Indra promised: Arjuna will spend it *as a dancer without manhood*, the curse Urvashi laid on him in heaven, and teach the dances he learned from Chitrasena.
::

***

::scene{#s5 frame=nakula,sahadeva light=nakula}

[Nakula](nakula) would be Granthika, keeper of Virata's horses, under whose hand even wild colts grow gentle. [Sahadeva](sahadeva) would be Tantripala, keeper of the king's cattle, who knew every sign on a cow and every bull of good omen. {#p7 4.3}

***

::scene{#s6 frame=draupadi,yudhishthira,sudeshna light=draupadi}

And Draupadi? She had never done a servant's work, said Yudhishthira; since her birth she had known only garlands and perfume and fine clothes. {#p8 4.3}

[Draupadi](draupadi) answered calmly. She would go as a Sairandhri, a free woman who serves in other households, skilled in dressing hair, and serve Queen [Sudeshna](sudeshna). *Do not grieve so, O king.* {#p9 4.3}

> Do thou, therefore, conduct thyself in such a way that sinful men of evil hearts may not be gladdened by gazing at thee. {#q3 4.3}
> — Yudhishthira

***

::scene{#s7 frame=dhaumya,yudhishthira light=dhaumya}

Yudhishthira sent their charioteers and cooks and Draupadi's maids to Drupada, the empty chariots to Dvaraka, and told them all to say they did not know where the Pandavas had gone. [Dhaumya](dhaumya) was to keep their sacred fires at Drupada's court. {#p10 4.4}

Then Dhaumya gave them advice on living in a king's house. Wait at the gate for permission. Never sit where someone else wants to sit, or ride the king's chariot or elephant because you think yourself a favourite. Never give advice unasked. Speak softly before the king, don't laugh too loud, and don't be too solemn either. Praise the king in his absence as well as his presence. *To dwell with a king is, alas, difficult.* {#p11 4.4}
