Fearlessness, purity of heart, steadiness in knowledge and yoga, giving, self-control, sacrifice, study, austerity, uprightness, harmlessness, truth, freedom from anger, renunciation, peace, not finding fault, compassion for all creatures, freedom from greed, gentleness, modesty, steadiness, vigour, forgiveness, firmness, cleanliness, freedom from malice and from pride: these, [Krishna](krishna) said, belong to one born to the godlike nature. Hypocrisy, pride, conceit, anger, harshness and ignorance belong to one born to the demonic. The one leads to freedom, the other to bondage. {#p1 6.40}

> Grieve not, O son of Pandu, for thou art born to god-like possessions. {#q1 6.40}
> — Krishna

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna light=krishna}

The demonic know neither what to do nor what to leave undone; there is no purity in them, no good conduct, no truth. They say the world has no truth, no ground and no lord, and was born of lust alone. Holding that view, these lost souls of fierce deeds rise up as enemies, for the destruction of the world. Bound by a hundred nooses of hope, given to lust and anger, they think: {#p2 6.40}

> This wealth I have,—This (wealth) will be mine in addition,—This foe hath been slain by me,—I will slay even others,—I am lord,—I am the enjoyer. {#q2 6.40}
> — Krishna

*Who else is like me? I will sacrifice, I will give, I will rejoice*: so they think, deluded, and fall into a foul hell. Krishna casts these cruel haters again and again into demonic wombs, and without reaching him they sink lower still. {#p3 6.40}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,arjuna light=arjuna}

> Three-fold is the way to hell, ruinous to the self, viz., lust, wrath, likewise avarice. Therefore, these three, one should renounce. {#q3 6.40}
> — Krishna

Freed from these three gates of darkness, a man works out his good and reaches the highest goal. Whoever throws aside the scriptures and acts on desire reaches neither perfection nor happiness. So let the scriptures, Krishna told [Arjuna](arjuna), be his guide in what to do and what to leave undone. {#p4 6.40}
