Now, [Krishna](krishna) said to [Arjuna](arjuna), who was without envy, he would tell the most secret knowledge, the *royal science, a royal mystery*, easy to practise and imperishable. {#p1 6.33}

The whole universe is pervaded by him in his unmanifest form; all beings are in him, but he is not in them, as the wind moves everywhere and yet always rests in space. At the end of an age all beings return into his nature, and at the beginning he sends them forth again, and the work does not bind him, for he sits apart, unconcerned. Fools despise him when he has taken a human body, not knowing what he is. {#p2 6.33}

***

::scene{#s2 frame=krishna light=krishna}

He is the sacrifice and the offering, the herb, the mantra, the ghee and the fire; the father of the world and its mother, the grandsire; *Om*, the Rik, the Saman and the Yajus; the goal, the friend, the refuge, the beginning and the end, the imperishable seed. He gives heat and holds back the rain; he is immortality and death, being and not-being. {#p3 6.33}

Those who sacrifice for heaven go there, enjoy it, and when their merit is spent come back to the mortal world. Those who worship other gods worship him too, though not in the right way. {#p4 6.33}

> They who offer me with reverence, leaf, flower, fruit, water—that offered with reverence, I accept from him whose self is pure. {#q1 6.33}
> — Krishna

Whatever you do, eat, offer, give or suffer, make it an offering to me, he said, and you will be freed from the bonds of good and evil fruit. {#p5 6.33}

***

::scene{#s3 frame=krishna,arjuna light=arjuna}

> I am alike to all creatures; there is none hateful to me, none dear. They, however, who worship me with reverence are in me and I also am in them. {#q2 6.33}
> — Krishna

Even a man of very wicked conduct who worships him alone should be thought good, for he has resolved rightly; he soon becomes righteous. No devotee of Krishna is ever lost. Even those of low birth, women, Vaishyas and Shudras, reach the highest goal by taking refuge in him. Fix your mind on me, he said; be my devotee, bow to me, and you will come to me. {#p6 6.33}
